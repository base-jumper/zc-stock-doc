# NYKD.OL — Nykode Therapeutics AS

## Business Overview

Nykode is a Norwegian clinical-stage biotechnology company developing targeted immunotherapies using an antigen-presenting-cell vaccine platform. The lead asset is **abi-suva** (formerly VB10.16), a wholly owned off-the-shelf DNA-based therapeutic cancer vaccine targeting HPV16-positive cancers. Its current lead indication is first-line recurrent/metastatic HPV16-positive, PD-L1-positive head and neck squamous-cell carcinoma, tested in combination with pembrolizumab in the randomized Phase 2 **Abili-T** trial.

The business is pre-commercial: 2025 contract revenue fell to zero after the Genentech agreement ended in Q4 2024, and current value is almost entirely cash, platform IP, clinical data, and the probability-weighted value of future partnering/commercialization. Q1 2026 cash was USD 51.3m, with management guiding runway into 2028, or 2029 if the pending Norwegian tax case is resolved positively.

## Trait Assessment

### Downside Support / Valuation Floor — 0.65 / 0.75

At NOK 3.05/share, the equity value is about NOK 996m. The most tangible floor is liquidity plus balance-sheet value: Q1 2026 cash of USD 51.3m is about NOK 502m at USD/NOK 9.79, or roughly NOK 1.54/share; other non-current receivables of USD 33.3m, mainly the appealed Norwegian tax payment, add about NOK 1.00/share if recoverable. A conservative floor of NOK 2.20/share gives partial credit for cash plus a haircut to the tax receivable and platform residual value, implying ~28% downside to the modeled floor.

The floor is real but not hard: the company is burning cash, and the tax receivable is appeal-dependent rather than liquid. What would firm this up: the next quarterly cash balance, management's updated burn/runway, and any Norwegian Tax Appeal Board decision.

### Fundamental Stability / No Value Trap — 0.55 / 0.65

This is not an operating business with stable fundamentals; it is a clinical-stage option. The key fundamentals are cash runway, trial execution, and whether prior abi-suva signals remain credible. Nykode reported Q1 2026 cash of USD 51.3m and still expects runway into 2028; annual operating cash burn was USD 29.5m in 2025 and Q1 2026 operating cash flow was negative USD 9.6m. The clinical foundation is more encouraging: VB-C-02 showed an ORR of 29% and median overall survival of 24.7 months in recurrent/metastatic HPV16-positive cervical cancer, while VB-C-03 showed a 38.5% ORR in first-line HPV16-positive head and neck cancer versus a ~19.1% historical pembrolizumab benchmark.

The value-trap risk is that the apparent floor keeps shrinking through cash burn before the 2027 readout. What would firm this up: enrollment pace, quarterly burn through 2026, and whether Abili-T reaches interim analysis without material delay.

### Mispriced for an Identifiable Reason — 0.55 / 0.55

The market is discounting Nykode because it is pre-commercial, loss-making, and dependent on a binary randomized Phase 2 readout. That skepticism is rational. The differentiated view is only moderate: the stock trades close to book value and below the NOK 5.00 mean analyst target, while the 2027 Abili-T readout could convert the prior single-arm/historical-control signal into randomized proof-of-concept and revive partnering value.

The edge is thin because there are only two analyst opinions and no direct head-to-head efficacy data yet. What would firm this up: updated sell-side probability assumptions, peer transaction/readout benchmarks in HPV/cancer vaccines, and any partnership interest around VB10.NEO or abi-suva.

### Identifiable, Time-Bound Catalyst — 0.85 / 0.85

The catalyst is concrete and time-bound: Abili-T has regulatory approvals across multiple European countries, first patient was dosed in May 2026, and management expects the first interim efficacy analysis in 2027. The mechanism is clear: positive randomized data versus pembrolizumab alone would de-risk abi-suva, strengthen partnering and market-access narratives, and likely force a re-rating.

The main derailers are slow enrollment, weak efficacy versus control, or safety/tolerability issues in combination therapy. Signposts to monitor: site activation, enrollment updates, safety statements, and timing of the first interim efficacy analysis.

**Why the other strategies lost:** Cash-cow and wonderful-and-fair are vetoed by zero current earnings quality and zero free-cash-flow generation. Rule-breaker has some fit — large unmet oncology need and proprietary platform — but weak commercial traction, no revenue engine, uncertain top-dog status, and no customer-devotion evidence make it a poorer frame than a catalyst-driven freeroll.

## Overall

NYKD is a speculative, catalyst-led biotech setup rather than a quality compounder. The attractive part is the defined 2027 randomized data event backed by enough cash runway to reach it. The unattractive part is also obvious: no commercial revenue, no positive FCF, a shrinking cash floor, and high binary clinical risk. I would treat it as a small, event-driven watchlist position candidate, not a core holding.
