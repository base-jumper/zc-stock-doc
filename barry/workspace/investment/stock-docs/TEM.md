# TEM — Tempus AI, Inc.

## Business Overview

Tempus combines a clinical diagnostics laboratory with a healthcare-data and AI platform. Diagnostics generated 76% of Q2 2026 revenue through oncology and hereditary testing; Data and Applications generated the remainder by licensing de-identified multimodal records and providing clinical-trial, modeling, and workflow products to pharmaceutical and biotechnology customers. The loop is strategically coherent: testing and provider integrations add longitudinal clinical and molecular data, while data licensing and AI applications monetize and improve the usefulness of that corpus.

The platform spans about 700 direct data connections and 5,000 healthcare sites, reaches more than 55% of US oncologists, and contained 8.6 million de-identified patient records and more than 450 petabytes at FY2025. Q2 2026 revenue was $382.5 million: Diagnostics $289.3 million and Data and Applications $93.2 million. The main economic drivers are oncology test volume and reimbursement, higher-value MRD adoption, and expansion of life-sciences data contracts.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — 0.88 score / 0.86 confidence

Precision oncology is shifting toward molecularly guided therapy selection, longitudinal MRD monitoring, and AI-supported drug development. Tempus reported 31% oncology-volume growth in Q2, while MRD volume rose from 4,700 tests in Q4 2025 to 9,000 in Q2 2026. FDA approval of tumor-only xT CDx enables migration toward higher ADLT pricing beginning in 2027. The pending Personalis acquisition targets a company-estimated $20+ billion US MRD market that remains under 10% penetrated. The opportunity is large and structurally expanding, although reimbursement, regulation, and intense competition temper the score.

### Top Dog — 0.72 score / 0.74 confidence

Tempus has an emerging leadership position rather than uncontested dominance. Its network covers more than 55% of US oncologists, more than 65% of US academic medical centers, and 5,000 healthcare sites; it processed about 96,500 oncology tests in Q2 2026. Its dataset and life-sciences reach are unusually broad, including 19 of the 20 largest public pharmaceutical companies. Guardant, Foundation Medicine/Roche, Caris, Natera, Exact Sciences and other focused competitors remain formidable, especially in liquid biopsy and MRD, and Tempus does not disclose a clean boundary-matched market share. Confidence would rise with an independent 2026 US precision-oncology revenue/test-share study covering public and private competitors on one consistent boundary; add that source when available.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — 0.76 score / 0.78 confidence

The moat is the combination of proprietary longitudinal data rights, provider/EHR integrations, laboratory distribution, and a data flywheel: more testing adds matched clinical and molecular records that can improve applications and create more licensing demand. At FY2025 the corpus held about 1.4 billion documents across 8.6 million patients, including 1.5 million records with matched clinical and genomic data. Tempus generally retains de-identified data rights after provider contracts end, making replication slow. The advantage is credible but not proven to be winner-take-most; competitors also possess large datasets and well-funded clinical channels. Confidence would rise with independently reviewed, longitudinal evidence that model accuracy, diagnostic adoption, or pricing improves as Tempus-specific data scale grows.

### Evidence of Durable, Accelerating Growth — 0.80 score / 0.90 confidence

Revenue rose from $321 million in 2022 to $532 million in 2023, $693 million in 2024, and $1.272 billion in 2025, though the Ambry acquisition materially inflated 2025 growth. More decision-usefully, Q2 2026 revenue grew 22%, oncology volume 31%, Insights revenue 36%, and total gross margin widened to 64.4% from 62.0%. Adjusted EBITDA reached $8.0 million, but GAAP operating loss was still $75.9 million and quarterly free cash flow remained negative $18.6 million. The engine is strong and unit economics are improving, but headline growth has decelerated and true owner earnings are not yet established.

### Organic Growth over Acquisitive Growth — 0.48 score / 0.88 confidence

The core engine is healthy: oncology volume grew 31% and Insights 36% in Q2 2026. However, acquisitions are now material to the story. Ambry drove much of the 2025 revenue step-up; Paige, Deep 6 AI and OneOme extended the platform; and Tempus agreed to buy the remaining Personalis shares at about $1.5 billion enterprise value. The Personalis consideration can be up to 50% cash with the balance in Tempus shares, adding integration and dilution risk. Growth is therefore mixed rather than overwhelmingly organic.

### Great Management & Smart Backing — 0.66 score / 0.78 confidence

Founder-CEO Eric Lefkofsky has built Tempus from inception into a scaled clinical and data platform and communicates a coherent long-term mission. The board includes deep medical and technology expertise, and institutional ownership is substantial. Offsetting this are an aggressive acquisition cadence, related-party dealings with Pathos (also co-founded and chaired by Lefkofsky), and a mixed prior public-company record at Groupon. The unresolved question is execution through the Personalis integration; it is irreducible for now and should be revisited after FY2027 results show retention, synergies, and per-share economics.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.43 score / 0.92 confidence

Lefkofsky beneficially owned 21.4% of Class A and all Class B shares at the March 2026 proxy date, carrying 57.9% of combined voting power, so his financial exposure is meaningful but minority holders have little governance leverage. Long-term awards use three-year compound revenue growth and relative TSR, which is directionally sound. The negatives are material: the compensation committee paid 90% of the 2025 annual bonus despite missing the adjusted-EBITDA goal; 2026 stock compensation guidance is about $200 million; the equity plans contain sizeable evergreen provisions; diluted weighted-average shares rose 5.2% year over year in Q2; and executives, including the founder, have sold shares. The system rewards growth and stock performance more than durable per-share cash value.

### Customer Devotion — 0.84 score / 0.90 confidence

Demand-side evidence is strong. FY2025 Insights net revenue retention was 126%; Tempus served 19 of the 20 largest public pharmaceutical companies; and physicians ordering more than five oncology NGS tests had 87% 12-month retention. Q2 data bookings were about $200 million and oncology volume accelerated. Some stickiness comes from integrations and data gravity rather than pure preference, but expansion spending and repeat clinical ordering indicate genuine customer value.

### Why the other strategies lost

Wonderful and Fair and Cash Cow fail on demonstrated earnings quality and returns on capital: cumulative GAAP losses remain large, TTM free cash flow is negative, and Q2 GAAP profit came from a $98.5 million unrealized securities gain rather than operations. Freeroll fails because a 6.6× sales valuation, negative tangible book value, and ongoing cash burn provide no hard downside floor; operational execution is not a defined catalyst that forces a re-rating toward a measurable fair value.

## Overall

Tempus is a credible Rule Breaker with rare clinical distribution, a differentiated multimodal dataset, improving margins, and strong growth in oncology tests and data licensing. The score is capped by a still-unproven path to owner earnings, acquisition dependence, high SBC and dilution, founder control, and the risk that focused rivals win key modalities. The pending Personalis deal increases both the MRD opportunity and the execution/financing stakes.

## Valuation

At $52.05 on 7 August 2026, Tempus had about a $9.4 billion market capitalization and $10.0 billion enterprise value, equal to roughly 6.6× TTM revenue. The market model projects a $25.0 billion 2036 US precision-oncology diagnostics/data-AI market and assigns Tempus 7.55% mobility-adjusted capture, or $1.888 billion of terminal revenue. This surprisingly low survivor-path revenue is the discipline in the valuation: Tempus starts around fourth on the market's same-boundary ranking, and the pooled mobility model does not assume today's company-specific momentum persists.

## Thesis Connections

Fits the AI-enabled precision-medicine thesis: proprietary clinical data, integrated workflow distribution, and diagnostics form a feedback loop that generic AI vendors cannot quickly reproduce. It does not yet fit a proven-profitable healthcare compounder thesis.

## Watch

- Personalis close terms and financing: final cash/stock mix, new borrowing, dilution, and post-close MRD volume synergies.
- Quarterly oncology volume, therapy-selection ASP, MRD tests, and Data and Applications growth versus the 25% medium-term revenue framework.
- Operating cash flow and SBC: confirm cash-flow positivity by year-end 2026 without relying on working-capital timing or marketable-security gains.
- Independent, boundary-matched US precision-oncology market share evidence to firm up Top Dog.
- Longitudinal product evidence connecting proprietary data scale to improved diagnostic/model outcomes to firm up Sustainable Advantage.
- Revisit management execution after FY2027 results provide a full view of Personalis integration and per-share economics.
- Monitor the xT CDx migration to ADLT reimbursement beginning in 2027 and any payer/FDA policy changes.
