# SE — Sea Limited

## Business Overview

Sea is a founder-led consumer technology group built around three businesses. **Shopee** is a marketplace spanning Southeast Asia, Taiwan and Brazil; it earns transaction and advertising fees, logistics and fulfilment revenue, and some first-party merchandise sales. **Monee** provides consumer and SME credit, payments, e-wallets, banking and related financial services, using Shopee activity as an acquisition and underwriting channel while expanding off-platform. **Garena** develops and publishes games, led by Free Fire, and monetises through in-game purchases.

Q2 2026 revenue was **US$7.79 billion**, up 48.1% year on year. Shopee generated US$5.59 billion (72% of group revenue), Monee US$1.40 billion (18%) and Garena US$747 million (10%). All three segments were profitable on the company's adjusted-EBITDA definition: US$255 million, US$288 million and US$430 million respectively. The group therefore has a reinforcing commerce-and-finance ecosystem funded partly by a mature gaming cash engine, but Monee's rapidly growing loan book and Shopee's logistics and promotion spending make consolidated cash flow less simple than the headline numbers suggest.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — 0.82, confidence 0.90

Sea participates in three digitally migrating markets. The companion market model estimates third-party marketplace operator revenue across Shopee's core eight markets at **US$42 billion in 2026**, rising to **US$125 billion by 2036** as marketplace spend share and monetisation deepen. Monee addresses low credit penetration among consumers and SMEs and can extend from Shopee transactions to broader online and offline use cases. Mobile gaming is more mature, but Free Fire's scale and enduring engagement preserve a profitable global opportunity.

The deduction is for structurally hard economics: ecommerce buyers and sellers multi-home, TikTok Shop is scaling quickly, logistics and vouchers consume capital, consumer lending is regulated and cyclical, and gaming hits are difficult to repeat. The wave is large and durable, but competitive intensity can absorb much of its value.

### Top Dog — 0.88, confidence 0.92

Shopee is the clearest leadership evidence. Momentum Works estimates it held **53% of Southeast Asian platform GMV in 2025** and ranked first in all six major regional markets; Sea also describes Shopee as the largest marketplace in Southeast Asia and Taiwan and a leader in Brazil. Q2 2026 Shopee GMV reached US$38.3 billion, up 28.4%, and gross orders rose 27.5% to 4.2 billion.

Garena's Free Fire continued to draw more than 100 million average daily active users, while Monee's consumer and SME loans reached US$11.1 billion. Shopee's lead is meaningful rather than unassailable: TikTok Shop plus Tokopedia reached 65.7% of Shopee's Southeast Asian GMV in 2025, Mercado Libre remains the stronger incumbent in Brazil, and each country has distinct regulation and logistics.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — 0.78, confidence 0.86

Sea's moat combines buyer-seller network effects, parcel density, advertising and transaction data, local logistics infrastructure, payment and credit integration, and a recognised consumer brand. More commerce supplies underwriting and fraud data to Monee; payments reduce Shopee checkout friction; logistics quality and seller tools improve marketplace conversion. Q2 core marketplace revenue rose 65.6% against 28.4% GMV growth, evidence that scale is supporting deeper monetisation.

The moat is widening operationally, but switching costs remain low and the company is still paying to strengthen it. Q2 Shopee sales and marketing expense rose 56.6%, logistics investment lifted cost of service 51.9%, and content commerce gives TikTok a differentiated acquisition loop. Sea has a durable regional advantage, not a captive market.

### Evidence of Durable, Accelerating Growth — 0.92, confidence 0.98

Sea is a maturing growth company, so revenue, GMV, bookings, loan balances and segment profitability are the relevant metrics. Revenue rose from US$12.45 billion in 2022 to US$13.06 billion in 2023, US$16.82 billion in 2024 and US$22.94 billion in 2025; growth accelerated from 4.9% to 28.8% and then 36.4%. H1 2026 revenue grew 47.4%, including 46.6% in Q1 and 48.1% in Q2.

The operating drivers confirm the breadth. Q2 Shopee GMV grew 28.4%; Monee revenue grew 58.9% and loans 62.5%; Garena bookings grew 15.5%, paying users 10.2% and bookings per user 16%. Group operating income rose 33.3%, although net income grew only 10.6% because marketing, credit provisions and tax rose faster than revenue. Gross margin held near 45%, but the ADS was down about 31% over the latest 52 weeks—price action diverges from operating growth rather than confirming it.

### Organic Growth over Acquisitive Growth — 0.93, confidence 0.95

Growth is overwhelmingly built internally. Cash acquisition spending was only about US$105 million across 2022–2025, goodwill was 0.4% of assets at end-2025, and the 20-F says no internally developed software costs were capitalised before technological feasibility. Shopee's order and GMV growth, Monee's loan origination, and Garena's user monetisation explain the current expansion without a roll-up.

The dilution fingerprint is real but comes from equity compensation and convertible financing rather than bought revenue: diluted average shares rose from 558 million in 2022 to 638 million in 2025. That leakage is scored under incentive alignment and valuation rather than treated as acquisitive growth.

### Great Management & Smart Backing — 0.88, confidence 0.90

Founder Forrest Li has led Sea since 2009. Co-founder Gang Ye remains COO, Chris Feng previously ran both Shopee and Monee, and long-tenured executives separately lead Garena, finance, legal and Shopee product. This is an unusually deep operating bench. Management survived the 2022 funding shock by pivoting from subsidy-led expansion to profitability, then resumed faster growth without returning the group to losses. The recent communication is operationally specific—GMV, orders, monetisation, credit quality, paying users and segment earnings—rather than built around adjusted narratives alone.

Backers include Tencent, Baillie Gifford, WCM and Temasek-linked capital. The main deduction is governance: founder control is far stronger than the economic stake and reduces the board's ability to check a bad strategic turn.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.35, confidence 0.98

Economic ownership is meaningful, but the system is too founder-dominated to score well. The 2025 20-F reports Forrest Li beneficially owned 16.0% including exercisable awards and voting proxies, yet controlled **57.6% of voting power** through 15-vote Class B shares and proxies. He also chairs the compensation and nominating committees; only three of seven directors are independent, and Sea follows Cayman home-country practice instead of NYSE requirements for majority-independent boards and fully independent compensation and nomination committees.

The 2009 share plan automatically added 3% of outstanding shares annually through 2026, authorised up to 246.9 million shares, and had 37.4 million options plus 7.7 million RSUs outstanding at March 2026. Stock compensation was US$625 million in 2025, and most award recipients grant Li an irrevocable voting proxy. H1 2026 repurchases of 6.5 million shares for US$585 million partially offset dilution, but repeated insider sales and historical share-count growth mean granted equity has not yet produced clean per-share alignment.

### Customer Devotion — 0.80, confidence 0.85

The strongest evidence is repeated voluntary use. Shopee's Q2 orders grew 27.5%, Free Fire retained more than 100 million average daily active users, and Garena's paying-user ratio rose from 9.3% to 10.2%. On 16 August 2026, the Singapore Shopee Android app rated 4.6/5 from 213,000 reviews with 5 million-plus downloads and ranked number two among free shopping apps; Free Fire rated 4.3/5 from 129 million reviews, exceeded 1 billion downloads and ranked number two among top-grossing action games in the U.S. Play store.

This is preference and habit rather than contractual lock-in. The deduction is that Shopee still uses heavy promotions and logistics investment to sustain engagement, while marketplace users can switch apps cheaply. Sea does not disclose Shopee cohort retention, buyer frequency or seller net revenue retention, so devotion is strong but not independently separable from incentives.

### Why the other strategies lost

Wonderful-and-fair and cash-cow were ruled out by the short and volatile profit record: Sea lost US$1.65 billion in 2022, only became profitable in 2023, and its headline free cash flow is inflated as a measure of distributable owner earnings because Monee loan growth sits in investing cash flow while customer deposits sit in financing. ROIC improved to 11.5% in 2025 but has not yet demonstrated a high through-cycle level. Cash-cow is additionally inconsistent with Sea's heavy reinvestment and lack of a dividend. Freeroll was ruled out because net cash is only about 7% of the share price, the ADS trades above five times tangible book, and no specific ≤4-year catalyst forces recognition of fair value.

## Overall

Sea is a genuine Rule Breaker with three profitable digital franchises, category leadership, accelerating revenue, predominantly organic growth and a deep founder-era management bench. Shopee's marketplace scale and Monee integration create the most valuable compounding loop; Garena supplies high-margin cash and user reach.

The detractors are equally concrete. TikTok is closing the ecommerce gap, Shopee's current growth requires sharply higher selling and logistics spend, Monee's credit-loss provision rose faster than its loan book in Q2, Free Fire remains a concentrated gaming asset, and public shareholders accept severe founder entrenchment and ongoing equity dilution. The company is exceptional enough to watch closely, but the share price still assumes a large part of the success case.

## Valuation

The canonical Rule Breakers valuation blends two ten-year lenses and produces an expected annualised ROI of **7.68%**. Exit-multiple receives 65% because it captures Shopee, Monee and Garena together; TAM-capture receives 35% because its externally modelled market boundary is valuable but covers only Shopee's third-party marketplace revenue. The component spread is 2.49 percentage points.

**TAM-capture — 6.06% annualised.** The ecommerce market document estimates a US$125 billion market in 2036 and US$38.876 billion of mobility-adjusted Shopee revenue. Mercado Libre is the mature role model: its 2025 deep-history figures at roughly Sea's current revenue stage showed a 19.8% EBITDA margin, while a 20× terminal EV/EBITDA is below its current 25.9× multiple. Applying those assumptions, 634.5 million diluted shares and 1% annual dilution produces US$154.0 billion of terminal enterprise and equity value, or US$219.66 per share. Value creation contributes 7.12% annually and dilution subtracts 0.99%. Net debt is set to zero because this terminal value excludes Monee and Garena; importing current group cash or financial-services funding would mix boundaries. The result is a Shopee-only survivor value, not an unconditional group forecast.

**Exit-multiple — 8.56% annualised.** The current owner-earnings anchor is US$2.90 per share of GAAP earnings, triangulated between trailing EPS of about US$2.59, H1 2026 annualised diluted EPS of US$2.74, and the higher forward run-rate as current investments mature. Earnings are cleaner than headline free cash flow because loans and deposits distort consolidated cash-flow classification. Aggregate earnings growth fades from 24% to 9% over ten years, a 15.5% CAGR, with 1% annual dilution. A 25× exit P/E—below Mercado Libre's 32.5× forward P/E and close to Amazon's 25.2×—compresses from a 42.0× entry multiple. Business growth contributes 15.49% annually, dilution subtracts 0.99% and multiple compression subtracts 5.07%, producing US$277.14 per share.

At **US$121.94 on 16 August 2026**, the business quality is much stronger than the modelled return. The 0.47 agent score sits near the mechanical quality-value result: Sea may outperform if Monee becomes a major high-return profit pool or Shopee exceeds the mobility-adjusted market share, but a base case below 8% annualised does not offer much compensation for governance, credit, competition and game-concentration risk.

## Thesis Connections

Sea fits a Southeast Asia and Latin America digital-commerce thesis: mobile-first consumers shift retail online, marketplace density supports logistics and advertising, and transaction data enables adjacent payments and credit. It also has a practical AI connection through search, recommendations, advertising, fraud detection and underwriting, but the stock does not require an AI valuation narrative.

## Watch

- Shopee GMV, orders, core marketplace monetisation, logistics revenue and sales-and-marketing intensity; confirm the targeted US$1 billion of 2026 adjusted EBITDA is reached without slowing share gains.
- Southeast Asian GMV share versus TikTok Shop/Tokopedia and Lazada, and Shopee's revenue and unit economics in Brazil versus Mercado Libre.
- Monee loans, >90-day NPL ratio, allowance and provision for credit losses, funding mix and off-Shopee originations; flag provision growth persistently exceeding receivable growth.
- Garena quarterly and daily active users, paying-user ratio, bookings per user and Free Fire concentration; track Palworld Online and Monster Hunter Outlanders as diversification tests.
- Operating cash flow after deducting loan-book growth and the economic cost of stock compensation; do not treat reported FCF as fully distributable.
- Basic and diluted share counts versus the US$1 billion repurchase programme, option exercises, RSUs and the September 2026 convertible-note maturity.
- Any change to Forrest Li's 57.6% voting control, board independence, compensation-plan terms or insider selling.
- Q3 2026 Garena reporting after Sea removes deferred-revenue changes and related cost from segment adjusted EBITDA; restate comparisons consistently.
- Marketplace, lending, data, tax, product-liability and gaming regulation across the core countries.
