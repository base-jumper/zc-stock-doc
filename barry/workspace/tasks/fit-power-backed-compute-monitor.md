Goal: detect early signs that power, compute, or tokenised compute claims are starting to behave like strategic collateral, settlement assets, or proto-money.

Check at least:
- USD confidence / monetary stress: US budget deficits, debt-service burden, Treasury demand, reserve diversification, de-dollarisation, inflation volatility, gold/Bitcoin strength during fiscal stress, sanctions/capital-control shifts.
- Compute/power market structure: GPU-hour pricing, compute shortages, cloud reservation markets, AI companies acquiring power assets, data centres co-located with generation, energy-rich jurisdictions exporting compute.

- AI-agent settlement: signs of autonomous agents using non-fiat units, programmable payment rails, or compute-denominated credits.
- Major players: hyperscalers, AI labs, energy-rich sovereigns, exchanges/clearinghouses, crypto/RWA platforms.

Decision rule:
- If there is a material or interesting signal that should update the thesis, notify Nick on Telegram/current reachable chat with: what happened, why it matters, how early/credible it is, and whether thesis probability should move.
