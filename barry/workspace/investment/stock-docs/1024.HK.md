# 1024.HK — Kuaishou Technology

## Business Overview

Kuaishou is China's #2 short-video/live-streaming platform (behind ByteDance's TikTok/Douyin), monetised
primarily by **online marketing services (advertising)** and **live streaming**, with a fast-growing
**e-commerce** and **Kling AI** business under "other services". Q2 2026 (reported Aug 19 2026): DAU
412.3M (+0.8% YoY), MAU 797.3M (+11.5%); revenue RMB35.5B (+1.4% YoY). Revenue mix: online marketing
58.1%, live streaming 24.5%, other services 17.4% (e-commerce + Kling AI). Gross margin compressed to
51.6% (from 55.7%) as R&D spend rose +34.7% YoY to RMB4.6B on heavy AI training. Profit was RMB3.2B
(vs RMB4.9B a year ago); adjusted net profit RMB3.9B (11.0% margin). **Kling AI**, its video-generation
model, generated >RMB850M revenue in Q2 (+200% YoY), launched native 4K video output, and is
building a global tier-1 position. The balance-sheet is very cash-rich: RMB121.3B total available funds
as of 30 Jun 2026 — roughly 90% of the entire market cap.

**Kling AI** is the strategic option: a fast-growing, independently-funded video-generation business
(RMB850M+ Q2 revenue, +200% YoY, native 4K output). The Street views it as the main area of renewal
offsetting the core platform's slowing growth.

## Trait Assessment (Freeroll)

### Downside Support / Valuation Floor — 0.65 / 0.70

- **Floor type:** cash/asset backstop. Yahoo: price/book 1.59x (book ~HKD21.5), net cash ~HKD8.2/sh
  (24% of price via the script), dividend yield 1.75%. Per company, total available funds RMB121.3B ≈
  HKD ~133B vs market cap HKD ~148B — **~90% of the market cap sits in cash, equivalents and financial
  assets**. That is a hard backstop: a sell-off toward its liquid assets would be unreasonable.
- **Floor and downside:** a conservative, liquidity-haircut floor around HKD28–30 (below book HKD21.5
  and just above liquid asset value) implies ~12–18% further downside from the current price.
  The stock is ALREADY sitting at its 52-week low (~33.2) and ~63% below its 52-week high (92.6) —
  much of the de-rating has happened.
- **Durability risk:** part of the "cash" is fair-value investments that can mark down; the strict
  net-net (cash minus debt, Yahoo) is only HKD8.2/sh. The genuine floor holds, but a conservatively
  measured cash-only floor is thinner than the company's 90%-of-mcap claim suggests. This caps the
  score at 0.65.

### Fundamental Stability / No Value Trap — 0.55 / 0.70

- Kuaishou is **not** in ice-cube decline: revenue grew +1.4% YoY in Q2, and FY2025 revenue rose to
  RMB142.8B (the peak); platform margins are near 5y highs overall (FY2025 op margin 13.1%). The 2026
  profit decline is **investment-driven** (AI capex), not secular demand loss.
- But: live-streaming revenue fell -13.5% YoY, gross margin fell ~4pts QoQ on AI costs, and management
  guided to continued 2H headwinds (consumer spend shifting to essentials, e-commerce marketing
  pressure, fierce competition). The fundamentals are durable, but the new margin regime is not yet
  shown to be a floor — a margin trough whose stabilisation is unproven keeps this at ~0.55.

### Mispriced for an Identifiable Reason — 0.60 / 0.65

- **Consensus:** the market marks the stock down for heavy AI-investment math — margin compression,
  a slowing online-marketing macro, and weak DAU growth (only +0.8% YoY).
- **Our differentiated view:** the market is pricing a margin and growth trough as permanent, but
  Kling AI is a real, fast-growing asset (+200% YoY revenue), the balance sheet is ~90% cash, and the
  stock is now below even the lowest Street target (mean HKD70.3, low HKD47.8; Citi HKD72, Jefferies
  HKD82) vs the HKD34 price — a very large price-vs-target gap. The AI spend becomes an inflection, a
  clear differentiated read; part of the discount is, however, genuine China-consumer macro risk.
- **What changes the market's mind:** sustained Kling AI scale/profitability, online-marketing
  re-acceleration, and evidence the AI-spend intensity has peaked.

### Catalyst — Identifiable, Time-Bound — 0.55 / 0.65

- **Catalysts on a ≤4-yr window:** (1) **Kling AI monetisation inflection** — an independent funding
  round, +200% revenue growth, a credible path to a high-margin AI business segment. (2)
  **E-commerce / online-marketing re-acceleration** in 2H26. (3) **Buyback + cash dividend**
  (HK$1.97B bought back + HK$3B dividend year-to-date; total 2026 return to exceed 2025). (4) The
  wide Street target gap (mean HKD70 vs price HKD34) — a re-rating if sentiment turns.
- **Risk:** these catalysts mostly run at the 2–4 yr edge, and the market is skeptical of Kuaishou's
  AI-model economics vs ByteDance, Sora, Runway, Veo. Score ~0.55 (plausible, but not the tightest,
  highest-confidence trigger).

### Why the other strategies lost

- **Rule Breakers:** AI-video is a real expanding opportunity (right-place-and-time high), but
  Kuaishou's *overall* revenue growth is not durably accelerating (total +1.4%), it is not
  top-dog globally in AI-video, and the moat is unproven against intense competition — the
  durable-growth gate does not clearly clear. A genuine dual-fit, but the freeroll is the more
  defensible frame.
- **Cash-Cow / Wonderful-and-Fair:** the AI capex cycle undermines free-cash-flow stability; not a
  clean income compounder.

## Overall

Kuaishou is a deep-value Chinese platform: fortress cash, cheap multiple (~6.8x forward P/E), down 45%
from its 52-week high and below the lowest Street target, with a genuine AI option (Kling) providing
upside. The freeroll is well-formed — a real floor (cash), a real catalyst (AI monetisation / the
re-rating gap) — but the prize is capped by thin near-term growth, margin compression, and China-macro
drag. A defensible, moderate freeroll, not an obvious one.
