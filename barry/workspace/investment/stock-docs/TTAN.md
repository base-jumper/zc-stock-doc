# TTAN — ServiceTitan, Inc.

## Business Overview

ServiceTitan sells an end-to-end cloud operating system to trades businesses, initially HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors and increasingly other commercial and specialty trades. Subscription fees and usage-based products—including payments, financing and marketing—produced $925.4 million of FY2026 platform revenue; services contributed $35.5 million. The platform processed $82.1 billion of customer gross transaction volume (GTV). Revenue scales through new customers, customer growth, price/seat expansion and adoption of add-ons.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — 0.86 / 0.88

The trades are a large, fragmented and still under-digitised vertical; cloud workflows, embedded payments and agentic AI can replace disconnected point products. ServiceTitan estimates a very large addressable trades economy, while its $82.1 billion FY2026 GTV shows substantial room beyond its installed base. The main risk is that construction and repair demand is cyclical even though digitisation is structural.

### Top Dog — 0.78 / 0.78

ServiceTitan has a leading scaled platform in its target trades, about 10,800 active customers at FY2026 and an expanding product suite. Its >95% gross retention and >110% net dollar retention support a strong position, but the company does not publish independently verified market share and competes with Jobber, Housecall Pro, BuildOps and broader software vendors. Confidence would rise with an independent market-share study covering the core trades.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — 0.75 / 0.82

Deep workflow integration, accumulated operating data, payments/financing integration and the cost and disruption of switching support a moat. Retention above 95% and expansion above 110% are strong behavioural evidence. The moat is not impregnable: customers can use lower-cost vertical tools, and AI may lower software-development barriers.

### Durable Growth — 0.82 / 0.92

Revenue rose from $467.7 million in FY2023 to $961.0 million in FY2026, a roughly 27% CAGR; FY2026 revenue grew 24%. Q1 FY2027 revenue was $268.8 million, up 25%, while management raised FY2027 guidance to approximately $1.14 billion. GTV grew 20% in FY2026 and retention remains strong. Consensus expects growth to moderate into the mid-teens, making sustained 20% growth the key test.

### Organic Growth — 0.66 / 0.82

The core platform and add-on adoption are growing organically, but ServiceTitan has used meaningful acquisitions: FY2023 business purchases were $589.7 million and goodwill remains $860.3 million, about half of assets. FY2026 acquisition spend was a much smaller $19.8 million. Growth is not a pure organic record, although recent headline growth is not being manufactured by large current deals.

### Great Management & Smart Backing — 0.84 / 0.85

Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan co-founded ServiceTitan in 2007 after seeing their fathers run trades businesses and remain CEO and president. The long product build, successful IPO and improving operating leverage support execution credibility. The board/backers include Bessemer and ICONIQ representatives. Founder dependence is a real offset, but the two-founder structure and broader executive team reduce single-person fragility.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.53 / 0.88

Founders retain material ownership and voting influence, aligning long-term outcomes, but the dual-class structure weakens outside-holder checks. The issuance is neither purely executive enrichment nor a one-off. SBC was $102.5 million, $163.7 million and $197.1 million in FY2024–FY2026 (16.7%, 21.2% and 20.5% of revenue). Excluding the special co-founder awards, it was still $88.5 million, $148.7 million and $143.5 million: a persistent, company-wide cost. FY2026 service-vesting RSU grants totalled 3.33 million shares, normally vesting over four years; the CFO received 65,670 and compensated outside directors only 9,455 in aggregate, while the founders received no new FY2026 awards. This strongly indicates broad employee distribution rather than grants being concentrated in current executives, although the company does not disclose employee-level participation or median grants.

The major exception is the October 2024 grant of 3.24 million performance RSUs to **each** founder—6.48 million shares in total, or about 6.8% of the March 2026 share base if fully earned. It is unusually large, but genuinely performance-conditioned: only 0.29 million aggregate shares vest at a sustained $140 share price, with the rest requiring sustained $240, $340 and $440 prices by October 2034. The founders received no FY2026 equity because the compensation committee considers this multi-year award sufficient. At January 2026, $195.0 million of its accounting cost remained unrecognised.

The apparent jump in weighted-average shares from 42.1 million in FY2025 to 92.4 million in FY2026 is mostly an IPO artefact—the December 2024 conversion of 42.5 million preferred shares into common—not a useful ongoing dilution rate. The recurring burden is better shown by FY2026's 1.79 million service RSUs released, 2.03 million options exercised and 3.33 million new service RSUs granted. At January 2026, 16.36 million shares (about 17% of the then share base) sat behind outstanding options/RSUs, including the founder award, and another 25.65 million were authorised for future grants or employee purchases. Authorisation is not issuance, but the 2024 plan can automatically add up to 5% of outstanding shares annually and the unused ESPP reserve up to 1%, unless administrators choose less. Overall, the equity mostly functions as normal broad-based employee compensation at market grant-date values, but at an excessive rate for current shareholders; the founders' outsized award and generous evergreen capacity strengthen that conclusion. Recent founder and sponsor sales further temper alignment.

### Customer Devotion — 0.85 / 0.92

FY2026 gross dollar retention exceeded 95%, net dollar retention exceeded 110%, and active customers rose about 14% to 10,800. Those figures show customers stay and expand even as ServiceTitan increases wallet share. This is stronger evidence than review-site sentiment; watch whether retention holds as growth normalises.

### Why the other strategies lost

Wonderful-and-Fair and Cash Cow are ruled out by persistent GAAP losses and SBC-heavy cash generation; the make-or-break earnings-quality trait is zero. Freeroll is ruled out because there is no hard valuation floor or identifiable time-bound re-rating catalyst—the thesis is long-duration execution.

## Overall

ServiceTitan is a credible vertical-software leader with excellent retention, durable mid-20s historical growth and rapidly improving cash generation. The detractors are a demanding valuation, slowing forward growth, substantial SBC/dilution, acquisition-created goodwill and founder voting control. No reviewed filing indicated fraud, insolvency, a thesis-threatening hidden liability, abusive related-party leakage or an imminent regulatory kill-shot.

## Valuation

At $78.55 on 16 July 2026, the equity value was about $7.5 billion and enterprise value about $7.1 billion, roughly 7.0× trailing revenue. Because GAAP earnings remain negative and FCF is heavily supported by SBC, a bottom-up exit-multiple lens is not yet reliable; the weighted valuation therefore uses only TAM capture.

The base case assumes a $20 billion maturity-year revenue pool for trades operating software, payments and adjacent products; 15% capture; a 30% EBITDA margin; a 20× EV/EBITDA exit multiple; 3% annual dilution; 95.4 million current diluted shares; zero terminal net debt; and eight years. Intuit is the role model for a deeply embedded SMB workflow and payments platform, though ServiceTitan's narrower vertical makes the analogy imperfect. These assumptions are deliberately exposed: TAM boundary and capture are the weakest inputs. The script produces an 8.33% annualised ROI (1.90× total): 11.58% annualised value creation less a 2.91% dilution drag. That is modest compensation for the execution risk, so the 0.68 agent score reflects high business quality offset by valuation sensitivity and dilution.

## Thesis Connections

Potential fit with a thesis on vertical software platforms using embedded fintech and proprietary workflow data to deploy agentic AI.

## Watch

- Q2 FY2027 revenue growth and any revision to approximately $1.14 billion full-year guidance.
- Gross retention >95% and net dollar retention >110%; deterioration would directly weaken the moat and devotion scores.
- SBC excluding the co-founder PSUs, service-RSU grants versus forfeitures/releases, and fully diluted share growth; target a clear decline from FY2026 core SBC of 14.9% of revenue and gross service-RSU grants equal to about 3.5% of shares.
- Annual additions to the 2024 Plan/ESPP reserves: the permitted evergreen increases are up to 5% and 1% of outstanding shares respectively, but actual additions can be lower.
- Co-founder PSU progress: 0.29 million aggregate shares require a sustained $140 price and the remaining 6.19 million require $240–$440 by October 2034.
- Independent core-trades market-share evidence to firm up Top Dog.
- GAAP operating margin and FCF excluding the economic cost of SBC.
- Atlas/Max AI adoption and whether it expands revenue per customer without weakening retention.
