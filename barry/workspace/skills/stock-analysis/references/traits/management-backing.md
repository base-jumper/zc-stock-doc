# Trait: Great Management & Smart Backing

**What we're looking for:**
The company is led by people who are exceptional — visionary, ethical, and deeply invested in
the business. Smart, patient capital (institutional investors who understand the business) is
also a positive signal.

**What to look for in management:**

* **Founder-led or founder-mentality:** Founders tend to think in decades, not quarters
* **Track record:** Has leadership navigated challenges before and come out stronger?
* **Communication quality:** Do earnings calls and shareholder letters show clear thinking,
honesty about setbacks, and long-term orientation?
* **Culture:** Is the company known as a great place to work? Does it attract exceptional talent?
* **Mission alignment:** Does leadership genuinely believe in what they're building?
* **Outward thinking:** Does management talk about new products & services, customer needs,
the value their solutions deliver, and the impact they are having on the world?
Avoid managers that only talk about internal metrics, like P&L, earnings beats, earning-per-share
targets, etc. If you can't tell what the company does by reading their annual report it is
cause for concern.

Note: **financial skin in the game** — insider ownership, whether stakes were bought or granted, and
recent insider buying/selling — is assessed under [incentive alignment](incentive-alignment.md), which
owns that read. This trait judges the people; consult it where ownership colours the management-quality
picture (e.g. genuine founder conviction).

**What to look for in backers:**

* Respected long-term institutional investors (not just momentum funds)
* Venture-backed companies where the VCs are known for patient, founder-friendly investing
* Board members with relevant domain expertise

**Key questions:**

* Would you want to work for this CEO?
* Is the CEO primarily managing for the next quarter or the next decade?

**Scoring guidance:**

* **0.70–1.00** Exceptional founder or founder-mentality CEO; clear long-term, mission-driven stewardship; respected backers
* **0.40–0.69** Competent but not visionary leadership; or smart backing without exceptional management
* **0.00–0.39** Revolving door of leadership; mercenary, short-term executives; no credible owner mentality

**Documentation:**

* If the company is founder-led, note it.
* Track record — prior outcomes and how they handled setbacks
* Ownership and insider buying/selling are documented under [incentive alignment](incentive-alignment.md); note here only where they bear on the management-quality read (e.g. founder conviction)
* Notable backers / major institutional holders and their reputation
* Signals from shareholder letters, earnings calls, and culture (e.g. Glassdoor)
