# 2533.HK — Black Sesame International Holding Limited

## Business Overview

Black Sesame is a China-based automotive-grade computing SoC and intelligent-vehicle solution provider. It designs Huashan high-computing-power SoCs for ADAS/autonomous-driving applications, Wudang cross-domain SoCs, and Hanhai middleware / toolchains, then sells SoC-based and algorithm-based solutions to OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers. The company is still in early commercialization: A1000-series chips are in mass production, but the model is not yet cash-generative.

FY2025 revenue was RMB822.3m, up 73.4% YoY, with gross margin of 41.0%. The annual results said Huashan A1000 products were the main chip-sales contributor in 2025 and had been integrated into models from Geely, Dongfeng, BYD and FAW, with penetration into global markets beginning. The company is also pushing into embodied-AI / robotics and acquired a 60% interest in Eeasy Technology, but the current investable case is still mainly vehicle-compute chips.

Current Yahoo snapshot: HK$11.95/share, market cap about HK$8.22b, EV about HK$18.0b, FY/TTM revenue RMB822.3m, P/S about 10.0x and EV/revenue about 21.9x. Cash was about RMB1.53b and total debt about RMB0.78b, but free cash flow remains deeply negative.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — score 0.78, confidence 0.75

The industry setup is genuinely attractive. The prospectus cites Frost & Sullivan estimating the global automotive-grade SoC market growing from RMB57.9b in 2023 to RMB205.3b in 2028, a 28.8% CAGR, while ADAS/ADS and cross-domain vehicle compute are structurally pulled by vehicle electrification, software-defined cars and higher automation. This is a large, underpenetrated market with real growth drivers, not a mature replacement market.

The mark-down is for competition and economics: automotive semiconductors have long qualification cycles, powerful OEM customers, and intense competition from Horizon Robotics, HiSilicon, Nvidia, Qualcomm and other better-capitalized players. Confidence would firm up with an updated independent market-share report post-2025.

### Top Dog — score 0.52, confidence 0.70

Black Sesame is a credible contender, not a clear global top dog. At IPO it claimed to be the third-largest provider globally by 2023 shipments of automotive-grade high-computing-power SoCs, and as of the prospectus had 23 vehicle-model design wins with 16 OEMs/Tier-1s plus partnerships across FAW, Dongfeng, JAC, ECARX, Baidu, Bosch, ZF and others. 2025 results add mass-production integration into Geely, Dongfeng, BYD and FAW models.

That is real traction, but dominance is not established. Customer concentration, production volumes, attach rates and share gains versus Horizon/Nvidia/Qualcomm are not disclosed clearly enough to underwrite a winner-takes-a-lot position. The next update should look for unit shipments, design-win conversion, and market-share trend.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — score 0.48, confidence 0.62

The moat is plausible but still thin. Positives: proprietary SoC/IP, automotive-grade qualification, middleware/toolchain integration, long OEM qualification cycles and switching costs once a chip platform is designed into a model. Gross margin recovered/stabilized at 41.0% in FY2025, supporting some product value.

Negatives: the company is still subscale, burns cash heavily, and competes in a brutal chip market where stronger players can spend more on R&D and ecosystem support. The current advantage looks more like technical credibility plus design-in friction than a proven structural moat. Confidence is moderate because detailed win/loss and pricing data are unavailable.

### Evidence of Durable, Accelerating Growth — score 0.62, confidence 0.76

Revenue growth is strong but uneven. FY2025 revenue grew 73.4% to RMB822.3m after 1H2025 revenue grew 40.4% to RMB252.9m; the second-half step-up suggests production ramps were meaningful. A1000-series products were mass-produced and shipped, and the company reported new domestic and international designated vehicle projects.

The issue is quality of growth: 1H2025 gross margin compressed to 24.8% from 50.0% before recovering to 41.0% for the full year, and FY2025 adjusted net loss was still RMB1.08b with negative FCF. This is deliberate growth-stage reinvestment only if revenue keeps scaling and gross margin holds; otherwise it is expensive cash burn.

### Organic Growth over Acquisitive Growth — score 0.72, confidence 0.70

The core growth to date appears mostly organic: A1000 mass production, OEM design wins and SoC/software platform expansion. The main caveat is the pending 60% acquisition of Eeasy Technology, intended to expand from automotive-grade to consumer-grade/robotics and broader intelligent-device chips. It may be strategically adjacent, but it is also a sizable use of capital for a loss-making company.

Score stays positive because FY2025 revenue acceleration appears driven primarily by the existing Huashan/A1000 platform, not bought revenue. Confidence would improve once the Eeasy acquisition closes and the company discloses organic versus acquired contribution.

### Great Management & Smart Backing — score 0.68, confidence 0.70

The company is founder-led. Mr. Shan Jizhang is founder, chairman and CEO; Mr. Liu Weihong is founder, executive director and president. The prospectus describes a technical leadership base in microelectronics and image-processing/automotive HDR, and the board/backer history includes venture investors with semiconductor/technology exposure. Management has taken the company from R&D to mass-production design wins, which is non-trivial.

The mark-down is that the public-company operating record is short and the business has not yet proven capital discipline through a full cycle. Founder control is a management-quality positive but also creates governance/key-person risk.

### Incentive Alignment — score 0.42, confidence 0.55

Alignment is mixed. Founder ownership and voting control create skin in the game, and the IPO prospectus said Mr. Shan controlled voting rights through trust arrangements. But shareholders have already absorbed meaningful dilution: 53.65m new shares were placed in February 2025 at HK$23.20, and share-based compensation remained material at RMB349.0m in FY2025. The share count rose from 569.2m at FY2024 to 640.9m at FY2025.

For an early-stage chip company, dilution can be rational if it funds R&D and design-win conversion; at the current burn rate it is still a real owner cost. Confidence is low because full post-listing remuneration detail and insider transaction history need deeper review.

### Customer Devotion — score 0.50, confidence 0.50

For this B2B model, the best devotion signals are repeat design wins, expansion across OEM platforms, and willingness by OEMs/Tier-1s to put Black Sesame silicon into production vehicles. The company has named collaborations with Geely, BYD, Dongfeng and FAW, plus A1000 mass-production shipment evidence, which suggests customers see the product as useful enough to qualify and deploy.

But devotion is not proven. Customer retention rates in the prospectus were modest in the early years, and there is limited disclosure on renewal, volume expansion by cohort, NRR, or whether customers would expand if switching costs were lower. Treat this as respected/credible rather than loved.

**Why other strategies lost:** Wonderful-and-fair and cash-cow fail because Black Sesame has no demonstrated consistent earnings, positive FCF, or high returns on capital. Freeroll fails because there is no hard valuation floor near the current price and no time-bound re-rating catalyst; this is a long-duration execution bet, not a floor-plus-catalyst setup.

## Overall

Black Sesame is a real rule-breaker candidate, but a risky one. The good version is clear: a domestic automotive-compute champion converts design wins into large production volumes, expands from ADAS into cross-domain chips and robotics/embodied-AI compute, and operating leverage turns a high gross-margin platform into a profitable semiconductor business. The bad version is equally clear: it remains a subscale chip supplier squeezed by giant competitors and powerful OEM customers, burning cash and diluting shareholders before it reaches profitability.

The stock-doc score is restrained mostly by valuation and proof. At ~10x sales / ~22x EV revenue for a still-loss-making, cash-burning company, today's market price already capitalizes a lot of success.

## Thesis Connections

Potential FIT: intelligent vehicles / edge AI compute / China semiconductor localization. Black Sesame is an exposure to domestic automotive AI silicon and possibly embodied-AI compute, but the thesis is not yet clean enough to be a high-conviction compounder because competitive position and unit economics remain unproven.

## Watch

- FY2026 revenue growth and whether full-year gross margin stays near or above 40%.
- A1000/Wudang unit shipments, design-win conversion, and named production models from BYD, Geely, Dongfeng and FAW.
- Cash runway: operating cash burn, R&D intensity, use of placement proceeds, and any further equity issuance.
- Evidence of competitive position versus Horizon Robotics, Nvidia, Qualcomm, HiSilicon and other auto-compute platforms.
- Eeasy acquisition closing, integration, and organic versus acquired revenue contribution.
- Any disclosure of customer retention, repeat orders, cohort expansion, or overseas design wins converting to production.
