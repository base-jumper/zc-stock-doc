# HMC.AX — HMC Capital Limited

## Business Overview

HMC Capital is an ASX-listed alternative asset manager and balance-sheet investor. It earns recurring management fees, transaction/performance fees, co-investment distributions and mark-to-market/fair-value gains from platforms across real estate, digital infrastructure/data centres, private credit, energy transition and private equity. At 31 Dec 2025 HMC reported $19.5bn of AUM, including $15.9bn fee-generating AUM, with 1H FY26 management fee revenue up 34% to $84.5m as real estate grew and DGT contributed a full six months of revenue. The company announcements page checked on 11 Aug 2026 shows no material operating release after the 30 Jun KKR/Illuma close; the only later release is a 15 Jul timetable notice scheduling HMC's FY26 result for 26 Aug. Current price used here is A$3.08, the 10 Aug 2026 close from Yahoo Finance.

## Trait Assessment

### Downside Support / Valuation Floor — 0.72 confidence 0.76

The floor is asset-backed rather than earnings-only. HMC reported FY25 adjusted NTA of $1.336bn, or $3.24/share, and statutory NTA of $3.61/share. Against the current $3.08 share price, I still haircut adjusted NTA by ~25% to a stress floor of ~$2.45/share for the payoff model. That leaves 20.5% downside to the modelled floor, wider than at the prior refresh, so the trait score falls. The floor remains asset-backed but not hard-cash guaranteed: FY25 cash plus undrawn debt was $680.5m, the May 2026 update cited ~$0.9bn of liquidity/dry powder, and the KKR close reduces HMC's invested capital in the energy platform to about $190m. Confidence would firm up with the 26 Aug FY26 result's updated NTA and listed/co-investment marks.

### Fundamental Stability / No Value Trap — 0.70 confidence 0.74

The business is not a clean annuity: 1H FY26 operating EPS fell sharply because 1H FY25 had large non-recurring transaction/performance and investment income. The underlying trend is better than the headline: recurring management fees rose 34%, AUM increased to $19.5bn, management reaffirmed FY26 pre-tax operating EPS guidance of >40c and DPS guidance of 12c, and late-June announcements directly support future fee-earning AUM. The private-credit mandates add A$375m of seed assets at financial close and up to A$1.35bn of total funding capacity, expected to lift private-credit AUM to about $3.3bn on full deployment. The value-trap risk is still that HMC relies partly on fair-value gains, balance-sheet investments and capital markets access, so recurring fee growth must keep replacing volatile investment income. FY27 underlying EPS disclosure remains the key proof point.

### Mispriced for an Identifiable Reason — 0.60 confidence 0.67

The market still appears to be pricing HMC as a volatile balance-sheet/platform story, with the share price well below its current A$4.62 52-week high. At A$3.08, however, the discount to FY25 adjusted NTA has narrowed to about 5%, so the cheapness and differentiated edge are less pronounced than at the prior refresh. The variant view is that the market still underweights (1) the asset backing, (2) confirmed institutional-capital traction in private credit and energy transition, and (3) the shift toward higher-ROE recurring funds-management earnings. The edge is plausible, though still execution-dependent and awaiting FY26 evidence. External analyst consensus was not independently verified in this pass.

### Identifiable, Time-Bound Catalyst — 0.78 confidence 0.82

Important watched items have been confirmed. ACCC and FIRB approvals were received for the KKR energy partnership, financial close was achieved on 30 Jun 2026, KKR-managed funds will invest up to $603m into Illuma Energy, HMC receives a $35m FY26 establishment fee and the platform should generate about $7m p.a. recurring fees. HMC Private Credit also converted the previously disclosed >$1bn advanced-documentation pipeline into $1.35bn of institutional mandate capacity, with A$375m of seed assets and deployment of the balance expected in FY27. HMC will report FY26 results on 26 Aug, the next dated checkpoint for NTA, earnings quality and FY27 guidance. The other proof points remain FY27 private-credit deployment/fees, Illuma's first BESS FID, DGT's Chicago sale and SYD1 reinvestment, ~$15m of FY27 run-rate cost savings, and the move to underlying EPS guidance. These should validate or weaken the recurring-earnings thesis over the next 12–18 months.

### No Thesis-Breaking Disqualifiers — 0.85 confidence 0.66

No thesis-breaking disqualifier identified as of this update. The floor is not cash-only, so asset marks, listed-stake values and platform execution need watching. Debt does not appear to be a near-term equity wipe-out risk: FY25 HMC had no drawn corporate debt excluding non-recourse fund debt, and management targets nil permanent core debt, though it uses the debt facility to seed investments. Governance/controller red flags were not found in the primary material reviewed, but this was not a full forensic governance review.

## Overall

HMC remains a freeroll-style setup: an alternative-asset-manager/platform stock with balance-sheet backing and identifiable re-rating catalysts, not a pure emerging disruptor. No material operating announcement has appeared since the 30 Jun KKR/Illuma close; the new information is the 26 Aug result date and a higher share price. At A$3.08 the capital-only payoff no longer clears the 12% ROI hurdle and the stress-floor downside is less tightly contained, while FY27 recurring-fee proof remains the swing factor. Agent score: 6.5/10 — thesis intact, but the entry is materially less asymmetric.

## Valuation

Entry price: A$3.08. Reported FY25 adjusted NTA was A$3.24/share; I keep a harsher A$2.45/share floor to allow for a ~25% haircut to adjusted NTA and valuation marks. Fair value and sell target remain A$4.00/share, roughly 14x estimated FY26 post-tax EPS of ~28c if HMC delivers >40c pre-tax operating EPS and the market gives partial credit for recurring-fee growth. Catalyst probability stays at 65% and the time window stays 1.5 years because the KKR close and private-credit mandates are confirmed, while FY27 earnings quality remains unresolved. The asymmetric-payoff script gives +12.26% expected return over the holding period, or +8.01% annualized ROI, below the 12% hurdle. This reflects 20.5% downside to the floor, 29.9% upside to fair value and only a 1.46x up/down ratio before dividends. The FY26 DPS guide of 12c implies a ~3.9% yield at A$3.08, not included in the model.

## Thesis Connections

Potential FIT: alternative asset managers with real-asset platforms where recurring fee growth is temporarily masked by volatile balance-sheet marks. HMC also connects to data-centre, energy-transition and private-credit themes, but the investment case here is mainly a discount-to-assets plus catalyst re-rate.

## Watch

- FY26 result on 26 Aug: update NTA, recurring-versus-transaction/investment earnings, FY27 underlying EPS guidance and the 12c FY26 DPS guide.
- Private credit: track deployment of the remaining ~$1.0bn dry powder, fee margin, credit quality and whether platform AUM reaches the expected ~$3.3bn.
- Illuma Energy: monitor first BESS FID in 2026, Moorabool CISA/offtake progress, and whether the KKR partnership delivers the expected ~$7m p.a. recurring fees.
- DGT: monitor Chicago sale completion, gearing reduction, SYD1 capex timing and FY27 FFO accretion.
- Floor risk: update adjusted NTA, listed holdings and balance-sheet liquidity after FY26 results; cut the score if marks or investments impair materially.
