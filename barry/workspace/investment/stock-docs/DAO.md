# DAO — Youdao, Inc.

## Business Overview

Youdao is a China-based, NetEase-controlled education and advertising technology company. Q1 2026 revenue was RMB1.35 billion: learning services RMB627 million (47%), online marketing RMB611 million (45%), and smart devices RMB109 million (8%). Learning services combine digital content, applications and education services; online marketing sells performance advertising using Youdao's technology and third-party internet inventory. The company is repositioning the portfolio around its Confucius large-language model and AI agents.

## Trait Assessment

**Disqualified — solvency/liquidity.** No strategy or trait score is committed because the universal eligibility gate fires before scoring. At 31 March 2026, current assets were RMB1.42 billion against RMB2.71 billion of current liabilities, including an RMB878 million short-term loan from NetEase; shareholders' deficit was RMB1.90 billion. Cash, restricted cash and short-term investments fell to RMB515 million from RMB743 million at year-end, while Q1 operating cash outflow was RMB93 million. Youdao explicitly states that its ability to continue as a going concern depends on executing its business plan, generating operating cash flow and securing external financing.

NetEase support reduces near-term failure risk but does not remove dependency: Youdao had also drawn US$118 million from a NetEase revolving facility. The facility was extended from March 2027 to March 2030 in April 2026, but the RMB878 million short-term balance remains larger than reported liquid resources. Minority shareholders therefore depend on continued parent funding before an investment thesis can be underwritten independently. ## Overall

The operating turnaround is real but not yet self-funding. Q1 marked a seventh consecutive profitable operating quarter and revenue grew 3.8%, driven by 20.9% online-marketing growth. However, operating income fell 44.7%, smart-device revenue fell 42.6%, gross margin contracted to 44.7% from 47.3%, and cash flow remained negative. The ADS rose about 60% over the year to $14.17 on 17 July 2026, leaving little reason to accept a financing-dependent balance sheet. `agent_score: 0.15` reflects the improving operations but overriding eligibility failure.
