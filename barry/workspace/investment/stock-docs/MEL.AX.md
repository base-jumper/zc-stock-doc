# MEL.AX — Metgasco Limited

## Business Overview

Metgasco is a micro-cap Australian oil and gas exploration/production company with a 25% non-operated working interest in the Odin and Vali gas fields in the Cooper Basin. The fields sell gas and liquids into east-coast Australian energy markets, with Vintage Energy as 50% owner/operator and Bridgeport as the other 25% partner.

The operating asset is real but sub-scale and declining. FY26 sales revenue was A$1.50m, down 35% year on year, and net production was 0.12 PJe, down 38%. The June 2026 quarter generated only A$292k sales revenue, down 20% QoQ, on 0.025 PJe production, down 17%. Reported 2P reserves at 30 June 2025 were 6.24 MMboe net to Metgasco, 97% sales gas, but production from Vali/Odin has repeatedly underperformed and the production uplift program has not yet delivered the hoped-for gas-rate improvement.

A proposed sale of Metgasco's 25% Odin/Vali interest to Vintage for A$5.9m was approved by shareholders but terminated by Vintage on 1 April 2026 after pre-conditions were not satisfied by the 31 March 2026 sunset date. The company is now seeking recapitalisation, debt restructure, new assets and/or other corporate opportunities while still holding the Odin/Vali interests.

## Trait Assessment

### Disqualifier gate — solvency / liquidity triggered

Metgasco is disqualified under the universal solvency/liquidity gate. This is not just a weak balance sheet; the evidence points to equity-wipeout/dilution risk before any operating thesis has time to play out.

Evidence:

- The FY25 audited accounts disclosed a FY25 net loss of A$3.27m, operating/investing cash outflow of A$2.67m, net liabilities of A$2.58m, working-capital deficit of A$3.72m and cash of A$1.12m, with a material uncertainty related to going concern.
- The 1H26 report worsened materially: cash fell to A$193.6k, debt was about A$6.08m, net liabilities were A$5.20m, working-capital deficit was A$5.23m, and the auditor again highlighted material uncertainty related to going concern.
- The June 2026 quarterly showed only A$366k of unrestricted cash, no unused financing facilities and an estimated 0.96 quarters of available funding. The reported A$1.616m cash total includes A$1.25m of restricted South Australian grant funds reserved for Odin-3/Vali-4 and unavailable for general working capital.
- Aggregate debt including capitalised interest reached A$7.0m at 30 June 2026. The A$2.0m senior secured facility and A$500k bridge carry 10% interest, while the A$3.18m convertible facilities carry 20% capitalised interest and had grown to A$4.5m including interest. Repayment was extended to 31 December 2026, buying time but not solving the leverage/cash-generation gap.
- The company says it is reviewing business-development and corporate opportunities to recapitalise and may raise additional capital. This is a near-term necessity, not optional growth funding: June-quarter unrestricted operating and development outflow was A$378k against A$366k available cash.
- The convertible loan terms include potential conversion at a 50% discount to takeover/capital-raise reference prices, so refinancing or survival funding could be heavily dilutive.

Because the disqualifier fires, strategy scoring stops here: Metgasco is ineligible for cash-cow, wonderful-and-fair, rule-breaker and freeroll frameworks until the recapitalisation/solvency issue is actually resolved.

### Why the strategies lost

- **Cash-cow:** no durable free cash flow; Yahoo/financials show negative FCF and operating cash flow, and production is not reliably self-funding.
- **Wonderful-and-fair:** fails balance-sheet resilience and business-quality floors; the company is dependent on lender support/recapitalisation.
- **Rule-breakers:** no evidence of a scalable category leader; this is a distressed micro-cap resource situation, not a compounding growth platform.
- **Freeroll:** there is asset optionality in Odin/Vali reserves and possible grant-supported wells, but the downside is not protected because debt, working-capital deficits and dilution risk can impair or wipe out equity.

## Overall

Score: **0.00 / 1.00**. The A$5m gross SA grants improve the chance that Odin-3/Vali-4 are drilled, but Metgasco's 25% share is restricted and cannot fund ordinary operations. With less than one quarter of unrestricted funding, A$7.0m debt and declining production, common equity remains a distressed recapitalisation speculation. The right analytical frame is not “cheap gas reserves”; it is “can the equity survive the debt stack and cash calls without punitive dilution?” Until that is answered, this should stay off the investable watchlist.

## Valuation

No ROI valuation calculated. A valuation model would be misleading while the solvency gate is active: any asset value has to pass first through A$7.0m of debt, Glennon funding terms, potential conversion dilution, cash-call obligations and recapitalisation terms. The 20% convertible facilities can convert at a 50% discount to specified takeover/capital-raise reference prices, so the eventual per-share claim cannot be underwritten before restructuring terms are known.
