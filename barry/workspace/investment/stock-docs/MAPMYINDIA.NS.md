# MAPMYINDIA.NS — C. E. Info Systems Limited (MapmyIndia Mappls)

## Business Overview

C. E. Info Systems, better known as MapmyIndia / Mappls, is an Indian location-intelligence and geospatial software company. It sells proprietary digital maps, Maps-as-a-Service, SaaS/PaaS location APIs, GIS/geospatial analytics, IoT fleet-management hardware/software, automotive navigation/ADAS/location products, logistics optimisation, drone/digital-twin tooling, and the consumer Mappls app/gadget ecosystem.

The business is unusually profitable for a growth-tech company. FY26 revenue from operations was ₹474.1cr, EBITDA ₹175.5cr, PAT ₹134.0cr, and cash/financial investments ₹685.0cr. The company also disclosed an open order book of ₹1,754cr at FY26 year-end, up from ₹1,500cr at FY25, giving several years of revenue visibility if execution and conversion hold.

## Trait Assessment

### Earnings Quality / Consistent, Predictable Profits — 0.70 / 0.86

MapmyIndia has demonstrated real profitability: FY23-FY26 annual net margins stayed very high at roughly 28-38% on Yahoo/YFin figures, and operating margins stayed around 31-40%. FY26 was not a clean straight-line year — revenue grew only 2.3%, EBITDA fell 2.5%, and PAT declined 9.2% to ₹134.0cr. The audited annual report also shows trade receivables rising 32.6% to ₹176.4cr; operating cash flow of ₹93.3cr covered about 70% of PAT and free cash flow of about ₹55.2cr covered only 41%. Q4 recovered sharply, but cash conversion and working-capital discipline now cap the score.

The FY26 annual report materially improves confidence. Customer concentration and quarterly collection detail would firm it further.

### High, Durable Returns on Capital — 0.76 / 0.78

Returns are strong but not extraordinary after accounting for the large cash/investment balance. YFin-derived ROIC was about 19.6%, 20.1%, 18.3%, and 14.9% across FY23-FY26; ROE was about 19.8%, 20.3%, 18.6%, and 14.8%. The decline in FY26 matters, but these are still good returns for a net-cash business investing in maps, software, IoT and geospatial capabilities.

### Pricing Power / Durable Moat — 0.72 / 0.68

The moat is primarily proprietary map data plus a long operating history. The FY26 release says MapmyIndia has built comprehensive India map data down to address and urban-building level, covering towns, villages and the road network, and continues to extend into 3D, 4D/near-real-time, HD, RealView and geo-demographic maps. That data asset should be hard to replicate quickly, and the company has served more than 2,000 B2B/B2B2C customers since inception.

The main uncertainty is competitive intensity from global platforms and well-funded local/government-backed mapping stacks. I have evidence of a data moat, not enough direct evidence of explicit price increases or churn resilience.

### Reinvestment Runway — 0.84 / 0.80

The runway is the strongest part of the thesis. MapmyIndia sits across automotive OEMs, enterprise digital transformation, government, logistics, mobility, IoT, EV, ADAS, APIs and consumer navigation. FY25 management reiterated a path toward ₹1,000cr revenue by FY28, and FY26 ended with a ₹1,754cr order book. Consensus on Yahoo/YFin expects FY27 revenue growth of about 24% and FY28 growth of about 23%.

The risk is conversion: FY26 annual revenue was nearly flat despite a large order book. The annual report disclosed FY26 new orders of ₹785.4cr, billings of ₹531.0cr, and a ₹1,754.4cr closing order book, but the gap between bookings and recognised revenue remains the central execution test.

### Capital Allocation — 0.66 / 0.68

Capital allocation is sensible but still being tested. The company carries a large net-cash/investment position, pays a modest dividend, and continues to invest in product depth, digital twins, AI, drones, international expansion and selective acquisitions/JVs. The FY26 annual report added a ₹2cr investment for 6.06% of IwayPlus and ₹2cr for a 20% interest in Prashanth Advanced Survey, alongside existing subsidiaries, associates and the Indonesian JV.

The score is capped because the business has moved into adjacent investments and acquisitions while FY26 growth paused. Those may be smart runway investments, but returns need proof.

### Conservative Debt / Financial Strength — 0.95 / 0.86

This is a major strength. FY26 total debt was only about ₹7.0cr versus cash and financial investments of about ₹685.0cr, current ratio was about 4.9x, and debt/equity was less than 1% on YFin. The balance sheet gives MapmyIndia room to invest through growth volatility.

### Key-Person Risk — 0.52 / 0.65

Founder/key-person dependence is meaningful. Rakesh and Rashmi Verma remain central to the company’s history, technology story and ownership structure. Rohan Verma's appointment as Joint Managing Director from 1 July 2026 makes succession more explicit and improves continuity, but also concentrates leadership further within the promoter family. Broader operating leadership helps, though the business remains founder-family anchored.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.78 / 0.72

Alignment is good. The FY25 annual report disclosed promoter/promoter-group ownership of 51.65% at 31 March 2025, while Yahoo shows high insider ownership. That creates a strong owner-operator signal. The counterweight is normal minority-holder risk in a controlled company: capital allocation into related/new ventures needs continued scrutiny.

### Why the other strategies lost

- **Rule breakers:** the industry setup and top-dog traits score well, but FY26 growth stalled and the company is already a profitable public compounder rather than a still-emerging asymmetric disruptor. It is a credible secondary lens, not the clean primary.
- **Cash cow:** this is a genuine secondary fit mechanically because the business is profitable, cash-generative and net-cash. I still choose wonderful-and-fair as primary because the investment case depends on reinvesting into a large location-intelligence runway, not harvesting a mature cash stream.
- **Freeroll:** there is no hard downside floor or mispricing/catalyst setup. At roughly 38x trailing earnings and 26x forward earnings, this is a quality-growth valuation call, not a cheap special situation.

## Overall

MapmyIndia is a high-quality Indian geospatial compounder with a strong balance sheet, high margins, good returns on capital, founder alignment and a credible multi-year runway. The main issue is valuation plus execution: FY26 showed that order-book visibility does not automatically translate into smooth revenue growth, and the stock still prices in a lot of future success.

The current view is positive but not table-pounding. The FY26 annual report strengthened visibility but also exposed weaker cash conversion, and consensus estimates have softened. It belongs on the watchlist if FY27 confirms revenue re-acceleration without further receivables expansion.

## Watch

- Whether FY27/FY28 consensus revenue growth near 24%/23% survives further earnings revisions; EPS estimates have already declined materially from 60-day levels.
- Segment growth by Automotive & Mobility versus Consumer/Enterprise/Government.
- Receivables and cash conversion: FY26 receivables grew 32.6%, operating cash flow covered ~70% of PAT, and free cash flow covered ~41%.
