# Trait: Capital Intensity

**Model role:** scored candidate trait; not selected by the current production model.

**What we're looking for:**
The up-front capital needed to reach minimum efficient scale. Large, lumpy, sunk investment
(fabs, data centres, networks, rolling stock) caps how many players can credibly enter and survive.

**Expected mechanism:** heavy capital requirements can limit the number of viable competitors and
thin the tail, but this trait did not add stable out-of-sample signal to the selected model.

**Distinguish from scale economies:** capital intensity is the *entry ticket*; [[scale-economies]]
is whether unit costs keep falling *with volume* once inside. A market can need heavy capex yet show
flat returns to further scale (or vice versa) — score them separately.

**Scoring guidance (0–1, how high the capital barrier is):**

* **0.70–1.00** Billions in sunk, specialised capital to reach viable scale; few can enter
* **0.40–0.69** Meaningful capital, but fundable by many mid-size entrants
* **0.00–0.39** Light — cloud-hosted, asset-light, or easily rented capacity

**Documentation:** the scale of minimum-efficient-scale investment, how sunk/specialised it is, and
evidence on how many players have actually reached scale.
