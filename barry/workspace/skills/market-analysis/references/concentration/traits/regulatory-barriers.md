# Trait: Regulatory & IP Barriers

**Model role:** scored candidate trait; not selected by the current production model.

**What we're looking for:**
Legal or regulatory gates that cap the number of participants: licences and spectrum, approvals
and certification, patents and regulatory exclusivity, or compliance regimes so costly that only a
few can bear them.

**Expected mechanism:** these gates can limit how many players are permitted or able to operate,
but this trait did not add stable out-of-sample signal to the selected model.

**Direction check:** score the barrier as it *limits entry*. Regulation that instead fragments a
market — franchise laws, forced interoperability, mandated unbundling — is a low score here, since
it *raises* the competitor count. Note that case in prose.

**Scoring guidance (0–1, how strongly regulation/IP limits entry):**

* **0.70–1.00** Hard legal cap — scarce licences, blocking patents, or prohibitive compliance
* **0.40–0.69** Real hurdles that slow entrants but do not cap their number
* **0.00–0.39** Open — no licence, weak/short IP, light compliance; or rules that fragment the market

**Documentation:** the specific gate (licence, patent, approval, compliance regime), how many
players it admits in practice, and its expected durability over the 10-year horizon.
