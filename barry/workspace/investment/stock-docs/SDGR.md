# SDGR — Schrödinger, Inc.

## Business Overview

Schrödinger sells physics-based molecular-design software and uses the same platform to run partnered and proprietary drug-discovery programs. Software is the recurring core: FY2025 software revenue was $199.5m, while drug-discovery revenue was $56.4m. The second stream adds milestone, royalty, and equity optionality but makes consolidated results lumpy. Customers buy on-premise licences, hosted subscriptions, maintenance, and services; the company is deliberately shifting toward ratable hosted revenue.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — 0.82 / 0.78

Biopharma is under pressure to shorten discovery cycles and reduce expensive synthesis-and-test iterations, while GPU compute and AI agents make large-scale predictive workflows more usable. Evidence of adoption is concrete: all top-20 pharmaceutical companies licensed Schrödinger software in 2025; trailing-four-quarter ACV reached $208m in Q2 2026, and quarterly ACV grew 27%. Bunsen extends the platform from specialist tools toward an agentic workflow and Bristol Myers Squibb agreed to deploy it. The market model estimates the global third-party software revenue pool grows from $1.5bn in 2026 to $8.0bn in 2036. This is a structurally attractive but still budget-constrained niche, not frictionless mass-market software. To firm confidence above 0.8, replace the model's reconstructed adoption series with a boundary-matched independent time series as one becomes available.

### Top Dog — 0.76 / 0.72

Schrödinger has more than 30 years of platform R&D, serves every top-20 pharma company, and had 27 commercial customers with ACV of at least $1m in 2025. Average ACV in that cohort rose to $3.9m from $3.3m, evidence that large customers are scaling usage, though the cohort count slipped from 29. The market model ranks Schrödinger first at about 13.3% of the defined 2026 vendor-revenue pool, ahead of BIOVIA/Dassault and Chemical Computing Group, but those shares are reconstructed because peers do not disclose clean segment revenue. To firm confidence, obtain independently measured, boundary-matched vendor shares or peer segment disclosures.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — 0.74 / 0.76

The moat combines a long-developed physics engine, proprietary workflows and know-how, deep integration into pharma R&D, and scientific validation. A 2026 peer-reviewed FEP+ study covered more than 230 compounds across five difficult targets; another workflow identified hidden pockets in the top predictions in nearly 80% of a 65-target dataset. Scale inside major pharma and accumulated validation raise switching and replication costs. The moat is real but not impregnable: cloud vendors, AI-native discovery firms, and well-funded pharma internal tools can attack the workflow. To firm confidence, review independent head-to-head accuracy benchmarks and renewal/expansion data by customer cohort.

### Durable Growth — 0.68 / 0.90

Schrödinger is an early, still-loss-making platform, so revenue and ACV are the right metrics. Revenue rose from $85.5m in 2019 to $255.9m in 2025 (about 20% CAGR), but the path was lumpy and included a 4% decline in 2024 before a 23% rebound in 2025. Software revenue grew 11% in 2025 and Q2 2026 ACV grew 27%, while H1 software revenue fell 16% because more hosted contracts are recognized ratably. Gross margin remains attractive but consolidated operating margin was deeply negative. Growth exists, but neither acceleration nor profitable unit economics is yet proven.

### Organic Growth — 0.90 / 0.88

The platform has been built internally: goodwill was only $4.8m at FY2025 and recent acquisition spend was immaterial relative to revenue and liquidity. The 2019–2025 revenue expansion therefore reflects internally developed software, collaborations, and customer growth rather than purchased revenue. Share issuance and SBC dilute owners, but that is scored under incentives rather than misclassified as acquisition-driven growth.

### Management Backing — 0.78 / 0.82

CEO Ramy Farid has been with Schrödinger for more than 20 years, president since 2008 and CEO since 2017; co-founder and Columbia chemistry professor Richard Friesner remains a director. The company has repeatedly converted platform work into high-value pharma relationships, including Novartis, Lilly, BMS, Takeda-linked programs, and Structure Therapeutics. The board includes experienced drug-discovery and finance operators. This is strong domain stewardship, though current operating losses and the choice to fund internal clinical work temper the score.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.57 / 0.88

The 2026 PRSUs use ACV, hosted-software adoption, and profitability over a multi-year measurement period; the proxy also provides a clawback, anti-hedging policy, and a CEO ownership guideline of 3× salary. The weakness is dilution: H1 2026 SBC was $17.9m and total common plus limited-common shares rose to 74.8m from roughly 60.0m diluted shares in 2020. The 2026 proposal added 3.0m plan shares. Incentives point partly toward durable operating progress, but substantial equity compensation makes per-share value creation a harder hurdle.

### Customer Devotion — 0.67 / 0.64

All top-20 pharma companies use the platform, average ACV among customers above $1m expanded 18% in 2025, and BMS agreed to broaden deployment around Bunsen. Those are meaningful preference/expansion signals. Schrödinger does not disclose NRR, gross retention, logo churn, NPS, or independent review depth, so switching-cost captivity cannot be cleanly separated from genuine devotion. Confidence is irreducible for now; revisit if the company discloses retention/renewal cohorts or after Bunsen's first full renewal cycle in 2027.

**Why the other strategies lost.** Wonderful-and-fair and cash-cow fail on persistent operating losses, negative through-cycle FCF, and negative returns on capital. Freeroll lacks a hard floor close to the share price: net cash and investments cover only a minority of the $18.16 quote, while platform and pipeline value remain model-dependent; Bunsen and the hosted transition are plausible catalysts but not a protected asymmetric setup.

## Overall

Schrödinger is a credible category leader with genuine platform validation, deep pharma adoption, and multiple ways to win through software and partnered molecules. The trade-off is severe: growth has been uneven, the core remains far from GAAP profitability, milestone/equity gains obscure operating economics, and SBC steadily dilutes the upside. This is a Rule Breaker, but not yet an exceptional one.

## Valuation

Exit-multiple is inapplicable because current earnings and normalized FCF are negative, so the Rule Breakers blend contains TAM-capture only. The validated [global computational molecular-discovery software market model](../market-docs/global-computational-molecular-discovery-software.md) projects an $8.0bn 2036 revenue pool and gives Schrödinger 10.3773% mobility-adjusted capture, or $830.184m of terminal software revenue.

The survivor case applies a 32% mature EBITDA margin and 25× EV/EBITDA, both below current Cadence Design Systems economics (32.5% FY2025 EBITDA margin; 43.9× current EV/EBITDA), to 74.806m current shares with 2% annual dilution and no terminal net cash credit. At the $18.16 entry price, the script produces a $72.83 terminal share value and **14.9% annualized ROI** (4.01× over ten years). Business value creation contributes 17.2% annually before a 2.0% annual dilution drag. This is conditional on surviving and capturing the modeled market share; it excludes proprietary and partnered drug-asset value, so pipeline success is upside rather than embedded software TAM. The 0.60 agent score reflects attractive conditional upside offset by execution, loss, and dilution risk.

## Thesis Connections

Exposure to AI-assisted scientific discovery, computational biology, and lower-cost drug R&D. The differentiated point is validated physics plus workflow integration rather than a generic large-language-model thesis.

## Watch

- Track hosted revenue mix (47% of Q2 software revenue), renewal behaviour, and whether ratable recognition converts into durable double-digit software growth.
- Revisit customer devotion after the first full Bunsen renewal cohort or any NRR/gross-retention disclosure.
- Monitor H1 operating cash use, SBC, and fully diluted share count against the stated path to positive adjusted EBITDA by year-end 2028.
- Track SGR-1505/SGR-3515 partnering, TAK-279 Phase 3, and milestone progression, but keep binary pipeline value out of the software market model.
- Review independent product benchmarks and boundary-matched vendor shares to firm top-dog and moat confidence.
