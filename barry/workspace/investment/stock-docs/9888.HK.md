# 9888.HK — Baidu, Inc.

## Business Overview

Baidu is China's leading search-and-AI company, dual-listed on NASDAQ (BIDU, 1 ADS = 8 Class A ordinary
shares) and HKEX (9888.HK). Q2 2026 revenue was RMB31.3B (-4% YoY, -2% QoQ): **Baidu General Business**
(GB) RMB25.2B and **iQIYI** RMB6.3B (-5% YoY). The core story is the transition from an internet to an
**AI-first** company — **Baidu Core AI-powered Business** was RMB12.5B in Q2, now **50% of GB revenue**
(+25% YoY): AI Cloud Infra RMB7.3B (+50% YoY, GPU Cloud +283% YoY accelerating from +184%), AI
Applications RMB2.5B (+3%), and AI-native marketing RMB2.6B (flat). The legacy side is shrinking fast:
Online Marketing Services RMB13.1B (-19% YoY, deliberate delay in AI-search monetization; management
guides ad revenue lower again in H2), Legacy Business RMB10.4B (-23% YoY). Apollo Go robotaxi did ~1M
fully-driverless rides in Q2 (23M+ cumulative, 28 cities globally). Balance sheet: **RMB283.1B
(~US$40B) cash + investments** vs a ~US$31B market cap.

## Trait Assessment

### Downside Support / Valuation Floor — 0.78 / 0.75

- **Floor type:** cash/asset backstop — a net-net-style floor. Baidu holds RMB283.1B (~US$40B) in cash,
  short-term investments, time deposits and adjusted long-term investments (Q2-26 balance sheet; company
  definition). Market cap is US$30.8B (HKD243B), so the cash pile is **~1.3x the market cap** — on paper
  the equity trades below its gross liquidity. Book value per share is HKD115 vs price HKD89.65 (P/B 0.78x);
  tangible book HKD93.7 (P/TB 0.96x).
- **Floor value & downside:** A conservative floor strips the investment portfolio to a haircut and
  values only cash + ST investments + time deposits (~RMB240B ≈ HKD264B ≈ **HKD97/share**), leaving iQIYI
  (RMB6.3B/q revenue), the operating business, Kunlunxin and Apollo Go for free. The stock is already
  **below** that conservative cash floor. A deeper hair-cut floor (e.g. HKD75, ~-16%) is the stress case.
- **Durability & mechanism:** the floor is durable — the cash is real, listed/ST investments are marked,
  and a new **US$5B buyback through Dec-2028 plus a maiden dividend policy** (announced Feb-2026) actively
  defend the price; US$259M already repurchased in 2026. The main erosion risk is continued AI capex burn
  (Q2 capex RMB11.4B) consuming cash faster than the run-rate, and FX.
- **Firm-up:** the 20-F/A (SEC) cash-and-investment breakdown with fair-value marks would firm the
  portfolio haircut (source: BIDU 20-F, cheap → Watch).

### Fundamental Stability / Secular Durability — 0.55 / 0.70

- **Trajectory:** total revenue flat-to-down (RMB134.6B→129.1B over 2023-25; Q2-26 -4% YoY); operating
  margin compressed 16%→8% (2024→2025) and net margin 17.8%→4.3% on the 2025 impairment (RMB16.7B asset
  write-down, largely iQIYI/one-off). Not a clean melting ice cube — but the **core search-advertising
  engine is in genuine structural decline** (-19% YoY, guided down H2) as AI assistants and chatbots
  displace traditional search queries, and Baidu is *deliberately* not monetizing AI search yet.
- **Diagnosis:** mixed. The ad decline is structural (category shift, competition from ByteDance/Tencent/
  DeepSeek-class assistants); the AI cloud growth (+50%) is real but margin-thin. The 2025 loss-of-earnings
  is a one-off impairment. Earning power is eroding at the margin — the "what's the stable cash stream?"
  question is unresolved while capex rises.
- **Verdict:** durable at the *asset* level, not yet at the *earnings* level. Score mid because the
  legacy cash engine is declining faster than AI replaces it, but the decline is gradual and the AI
  segments are growing off a real base.
- **Firm-up:** H2-26 segment reporting on AI cloud margins and ad-spend trajectory would firm this; watch
  Q3/Q4.

### Mispriced for an Identifiable Reason — 0.68 / 0.70

- **Consensus view priced in:** a Chinese search incumbent in secular ad decline, losing to AI
  assistants, with weak near-term earnings (Q2-26 non-GAAP EPS RMB7.22 vs RMB11.05 consensus, -35%; revenue
  -4% YoY, a miss). Forward P/E ~10.9x (US), P/B 0.78x — the market is valuing the *legacy* cash cow's
  decline, plus a China/geopolitical discount.
- **Our differentiated view:** the market is pricing the whole company as a dying search franchise while
  ignoring that **50% of revenue is now AI-powered business growing +25% YoY**, GPU cloud +283% (fourth
  straight triple-digit quarter), and that the equity trades at ~0.8x book *below its cash pile* — i.e. the
  AI businesses, iQIYI, Kunlunxin and Apollo Go come for free. The bull case is that the AI-first transition
  eventually re-monetizes (AI search monetization is *deliberately* deferred — an option, not a write-off),
  and the cash/investment value alone is an underwrite. Setup type: orphaned/misunderstood asset-rich
  transition + structural-ad-decline narrative we partly disagree with.
- **What would change the market's mind:** AI cloud margins inflecting up, AI search monetization starting,
  robotaxi/unit economics scaling, or the dual-primary listing bringing Southbound (Stock Connect) buyers.

### Identifiable, Time-Bound Catalyst — 0.72 / 0.70

- **Catalysts:** (1) **Dual-primary HK listing** — Board approved July-2026; EGM Aug-26-2026; expected
  effective **within 2026**; then eligible for Southbound Stock Connect, a structural buyer base that has
  historically re-rated Chinese internet names. (2) **Buyback + maiden dividend** under the Feb-2026 US$5B
  program (through 2028) — a capital-return floor that mechanically supports the price. (3) **AI
  monetization option** — AI search monetization, cloud margin expansion, Kunlunxin listing (process
  ongoing, per Q2 call).
- **Window:** ≤2 years for the listing + buyback (high probability); AI re-monetization is a 2-4 year
  option. Overall probability ~60% that the gap to fair value closes in ~2 years.
- **Confirming signposts:** EGM approval (Aug-26), HKEX approval timing, Stock Connect inclusion
  announcement, buyback pace, AI Cloud Infra growth holding ≥40-50%, ad-decline stabilizing.

### Why the other strategies lost

- **Rule Breakers:** Baidu is AI-first *in revenue mix* but the consolidated company is not a rule-breaker
  — total revenue is declining (durable-growth 0.40, near the floor-0 veto), top-dog is contested
  (0.55; ByteDance/Tencent/DeepSeek lead model mindshare), and durable-growth's FCF-tracking test fails
  (FCF turned negative). The AI segments are real, but the *whole-company* growth engine is not compounding.
- **Wonderful and Fair:** vetoed on **earnings-quality (0.35)** — profit collapsed in 2025 on a huge
  one-off impairment, FCF swung negative, and returns-on-capital (0.35) are ~2% ROIC — nowhere near a
  dependable compounder bar.
- **Cash Cow:** vetoed on **free-cash-flow-generation (0.30, floor 0)** — FCF is negative; the legacy ad
  machine is in structural decline, and while it pays a maiden dividend, the "durable harvest" definition
  fails.

## Overall

Baidu is a **cash-rich, asset-backed, AI-transition story at a deep value price**: ~0.78x book, equity
below its cash+investments pile, a 10.9x forward P/E, a US$5B buyback + maiden dividend, and a 2026
dual-primary listing catalyst that should unlock Southbound demand. The detractor is the *earnings*
picture — the legacy search-ad franchise is in structural decline (-19% YoY and guided lower), FCF is
negative during the AI capex cycle, and earnings quality is impaired by a 2025 write-down. The floor is
the strongest part of the thesis (real cash, actively defended); the risk is that the AI businesses take
longer to re-monetize than the market's patience, making it a "cheap for a reason" trap if the ad decline
outruns AI growth. Net: a well-formed freeroll with an unusually strong asset floor and a concrete 2026
catalyst.

- **Fair value:** HKD170.5 — consensus mean target on BIDU (US$166.7/ADS × 7.85 / 8 ≈ HKD163-170; US

Running the script writes the annualized ROI: **+21.5%/yr**, asymmetry ≈ 5.5x (upside/downside). The
return is carried by re-rating toward fair value plus the buyback floor — it is a catalyst-and-asset bet,
not an income one (dividend not modelled; method is price-only). `agent_score` 0.60 reflects a strong,
well-formed freeroll with an unusually hard asset floor, tempered by the structural ad decline and the
uncertainty that AI re-monetization arrives on schedule.
