# Trait: Data / Scale Advantage

**Model role:** scored candidate trait; not selected by the current production model.

**What we're looking for:**
A demand-side flywheel where scale makes the *product itself better*, not just cheaper: more usage
generates proprietary data or feedback that improves quality, which wins more usage. Distinct from
supply-side [[scale-economies]], which lowers cost rather than raising quality.

**Why it pushes `s1` up:** the leader's product is genuinely better because it is the leader, a gap
laggards cannot close by spending — so share concentrates at the top. Search relevance, ad targeting,
recommendation and fraud/risk models, and mapping are classic examples.

**Tests to distinguish real advantage from a data-moat story:**

* Does more data still move the metric, or has quality plateaued (data has diminishing returns)?
* Is the data proprietary and hard to replicate, or buyable / commoditised?
* Could a well-funded entrant reach "good enough" without the incumbent's data?

**Scoring guidance (0–1):**

* **0.70–1.00** Clear, compounding, hard-to-replicate data/scale-quality loop
* **0.40–0.69** Some advantage, but with diminishing returns or replicable data
* **0.00–0.39** Scale does not improve the product; quality is independent of size

**Documentation:** the specific feedback loop, evidence returns have not plateaued, and why the data
is not easily bought or matched.
