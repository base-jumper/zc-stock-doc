# Trait: Identifiable, Time-Bound Catalyst

**What we're looking for:**
A specific, identifiable event or development we expect to close the gap between price and fair value
within a defined window — ideally **1–2 years**, and **no longer than 4**. The catalyst is what
separates this strategy from generic deep value: a cheap stock with a real floor but *no* catalyst
can stay cheap indefinitely (dead money). We want a reason the market will be **forced** to re-rate,
and a clock on it.

Common catalysts:

* **Earnings inflection** — a cyclical recovery, cost-out, new capacity, or contract roll-off that
  visibly lifts earnings.
* **Investment returns** — capital investment or R&D expenses that were previously a drag on earnings
  are soon expected to start contributing positively to earnings.
* **Macro conditions** — an anticipated change in macro conditions is expected to provide a strong 
  tail-wind. 
* **Competitor underperformance** — the company is expected to gain customers from an underperforming 
  competitor
* **Regulation changes** — upcoming regulatory changes will provide a tailwind
* **Capital allocation** — buyback, special dividend, dividend reinstatement, a deleveraging milestone.
* **Corporate action** — spin-off, demerger, asset sale, strategic review, takeover / take-private.
* **Narrative resolution** — data that disproves the bear story (e.g. travel volumes recovering), a
  guidance reset, or the end of an overhang (litigation, regulation).
* **Re-rating triggers** — index inclusion, return to the buy-list, management or board change,
  activist involvement, or a disclosure that surfaces hidden value.

A good catalyst is **(a) identifiable and concrete** (not "sentiment will improve"), **(b)
reasonably likely**, and **(c) time-bound** — we name the expected window and what would confirm it
is playing out. A vaguer or slower catalyst can still qualify *if the floor is strong* (we are paid
to wait), but the tighter and more certain the catalyst, the better the bet.

**Key questions:**

* What specifically will force a re-rating, and through what mechanism?
* When do we expect it — and what is the realistic window (must be ≤4 years)?
* How likely is it, and what could delay or derail it?
* What early signs would confirm it is on track?

**Scoring guidance:**

* **0.70–1.00** A concrete, likely catalyst with a credible window of ≤2 years and clear confirming
  signposts.
* **0.40–0.69** A plausible catalyst, but with uncertain timing (toward the 3–4 year edge), lower
  probability, or somewhat vague mechanics.
* **0.00–0.39** No identifiable catalyst — re-rating depends on unspecified "sentiment," or any
  trigger is >4 years out or purely speculative. *(With a floor of 0 in this strategy, no catalyst
  means dead money — disqualified.)*

**Documentation:**

* The specific catalyst(s) and the mechanism by which they close the gap
* Expected timeframe / window, and why it is ≤4 years
* Probability, and the main things that could delay or derail it
* Confirming signposts to monitor — **feed these into the stock-doc Watch section**
