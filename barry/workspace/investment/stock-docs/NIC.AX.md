# NIC.AX — Nickel Industries Limited

## Business Overview

Nickel Industries is an ASX-listed Indonesian nickel producer. It owns controlling interests in four RKEF projects producing nickel pig iron/matte and the Hengjaya Mine, 10% of the Huayue Nickel Cobalt HPAL project, and 46% of the Excelsior Nickel Cobalt (ENC) HPAL-refinery project. ENC produced its first MHP in July 2026 and first nickel cathode on 11 August, moving NIC beyond intermediates into finished Class 1 nickel. The company has also agreed to acquire 36% of CNE and 17.5% of TMI while integrating ore supply through Hengjaya, Sampala and Siduarsi.

Profit is driven by LME nickel and NPI/MHP payability, ore grade and ore availability, Indonesian RKAB quotas, coal/electricity and sulphur costs, debt costs, and the ramp-up of ENC and future HPAL assets. The company reports in USD; the stock trades in AUD.

## Trait Assessment

### Downside Support / Valuation Floor — 0.58 / 0.70

There is some floor, but not a hard one. At A$0.825, NIC is below its A$0.888 50-day and A$0.905 200-day averages and 25% above the A$0.66 52-week low. Yahoo shows about 2.27x book, but the reported financials are USD and the ASX price is AUD, so statement-derived per-share asset values need an FX- and minority-interest-aware reconciliation. Using the A$0.66 nickel-downturn low as a practical trading floor implies 20% downside. The quoted trailing dividend yield is about 3.9%, but the commodity-sensitive payout is not a hard backstop.

Confidence remains below 0.8 because this is a trading floor rather than a conservatively reconciled NAV. A firmer score needs an asset-by-asset valuation of the Indonesian interests, net debt and minority claims.

### Fundamental Stability / No Value Trap — 0.56 / 0.82

The business is not structurally obsolete, but it is highly cyclical and remains exposed to nickel oversupply. June-quarter adjusted EBITDA from operations was US$120.5M: down 11% from Q1 but up about 40% from the prior-year quarter. Hengjaya Mine delivered a record US$45.7M as the revised Indonesian ore benchmark lifted realised prices, while RKEF EBITDA fell 30% to US$60.3M on planned maintenance and higher ore and power costs; HNC EBITDA also fell 30% as sulphur costs rose. The group generated US$82.4M before growth and debt payments and ended June with US$268.3M cash, but net debt remained about US$982M.

The diagnosis remains cyclical/temporary rather than secular decline. First MHP and nickel cathode from ENC validate the expansion path, but the durability of the earnings uplift is unproven until ramp volumes, costs and cash conversion are reported.

### Mispriced for an Identifiable Reason — 0.63 / 0.72

The market still appears to price NIC as a levered, cyclical nickel/NPI producer exposed to weak nickel prices, Indonesian policy, project execution and related-party complexity. The variant view has strengthened because ENC produced MHP and cathode on schedule and Hengjaya delivered record quarterly EBITDA, yet the share price remains below both major moving averages. The market may be under-crediting the earnings contribution from a successful ENC ramp, higher ore monetisation and Sampala/CNE/TMI integration.

The discount is still defensible rather than obviously wrong: June-quarter RKEF and HNC margins weakened, net debt was about US$982M, and ENC has not yet demonstrated nameplate output or unit economics. Yahoo's eight-analyst mean target is A$1.238 and median A$1.228, supportive but not independent proof of value.

### Identifiable, Time-Bound Catalyst — 0.84 / 0.86

The catalyst has begun to fire. ENC produced its first MHP on 21 July and first nickel cathode by 11 August, meeting the expected mid-August cathode milestone. The remaining 12–15 month rerating path is first cobalt sulphate by end-August, a clean ramp toward nameplate, visible ENC earnings and cash flow, and LME cathode registration. Hengjaya's 1.4Mt of July ore sales kept the company on track for its 14.3Mt 2026 RKAB quota; an application to lift the quota to 19Mt provides additional upside. CNE and TMI commissioning expected from mid-2027 is a second-stage catalyst.

Confidence is high because two primary-source operating milestones were achieved. It is not higher because the latest releases no longer repeat the earlier late-October nameplate timing, ramp costs remain undisclosed, sulphur and ore costs are elevated, and the CNE/Sampala transaction still carries approval and related-party risk.

**Why other strategies lost:** Cash-cow loses because earnings are volatile, debt service is heavy, and dividends are not the core reliable return. Wonderful-and-fair loses on low/falling ROIC, weak earnings quality, commodity pricing and leverage. Rule-breakers has some EV-battery/HPAL runway, but NIC is still a capital-intensive commodity producer rather than a dominant high-growth platform with customer devotion or a defensible demand-side moat.

## Overall

NIC remains a medium-quality freeroll/special-situation idea, but the setup has improved: ENC has crossed from construction into MHP and cathode production, Hengjaya is monetising higher ore prices, and the lower share price improves asymmetry. The case still rests on a 12–15 month rerating rather than ownership of a wonderful business; the next proof point is an economical ENC ramp, not merely first product.

Main positives: integrated Indonesian ore and processing footprint, record June-quarter Hengjaya EBITDA, achieved ENC commissioning milestones, Class 1 nickel diversification, and concrete 2026–27 operating catalysts. Main detractors: commodity exposure, roughly US$982M net debt, weaker RKEF/HPAL margins in Q2, major funding and execution demands, related-party complexity, and ESG/safety/policy risks.

## Valuation

I model this as a freeroll using the asymmetric-payoff method. Inputs are A$0.825 current price, A$0.66 floor, A$1.25 fair value, 65% catalyst probability and a 1.25-year window. The floor is the nickel-downturn/52-week-low area rather than a rigorous NAV. Fair value is close to Yahoo's A$1.238 mean analyst target and assumes ENC ramp and RKAB-driven ore sales become visible in earnings and cash flow. The higher probability reflects achieved MHP and cathode milestones; it still allows substantial failure risk around ramp economics and leverage.

The model gives 20.0% downside, 51.5% upside and 2.58x upside/downside. Probability-weighted expected return is 26.5% over the window, or 20.7% annualised; dividends are excluded. A$1.25 remains the sell target. My judgement score rises to 0.61 because the core commissioning risk has reduced and asymmetry improved, while debt, commodity and ramp-cost risks prevent high conviction.
