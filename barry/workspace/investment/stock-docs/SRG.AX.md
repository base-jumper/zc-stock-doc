# SRG.AX — SRG Global Limited

## Business Overview

SRG Global is an ASX-listed infrastructure services company operating mainly through Maintenance & Industrial Services and Engineering & Construction. It provides asset maintenance, remediation, shutdowns, specialist inspection, drill/blast, industrial/resources services, specialist engineering, civil infrastructure, water, transport, defence, resources, energy, health and education project services. Management says earnings are now about 80% annuity/recurring and 20% project-based. Profit drivers are contract wins, renewal quality, margin discipline, labour/input cost control, working-capital conversion, acquisition integration, and exposure to infrastructure/industrial capex.

## Overall

SRG looks like a high-quality industrial/infrastructure services compounder that has successfully shifted away from more project-heavy contracting toward long-tenure maintenance, asset integrity and infrastructure services. The business has strong momentum: FY25 was a record year, 1H FY26 was another record, guidance has been upgraded, and recent $1.85B contract wins give multi-year revenue visibility.

The problem is entry price after a huge rerate. At about A$3.75/share, SRG is no longer an overlooked contractor. It is being priced as a clean, recurring-revenue infrastructure services platform with continued flawless execution. That may be fair, but it leaves less margin of safety.

Current stance: quality business worth tracking closely, but not a back-the-truck-up entry at current valuation after the recent spike.

## Improvements

To reach a perfect score, SRG would need either a pullback to a more forgiving valuation, e.g. closer to ~9-10x FY27 EBITDA / low-teens PE, or more evidence that it can compound EBITDA at mid-teens rates for multiple years without margin, contract, or working-capital accidents.

## Portfolio fit

Useful fit for infrastructure services, industrial maintenance, water, defence infrastructure, and energy-transition services. It adds ASX industrial exposure but is still a low-margin services/contracting business, so it should not be treated like a pure recurring software or regulated infrastructure asset.

## Key Positives

- Strong growth track record: FY25 revenue A$1,323.3m, up 24%; underlying EBITDA A$127.1m, up 29%; EBIT(A) A$93.8m, up 43%.
- 1H FY26 momentum: revenue A$743.9m, up 20%; EBITDA A$71.0m, up 20%; EBIT(A) A$53.2m, up 26%.
- FY26 EBITDA guidance upgraded to A$164-168m and EBIT(A) A$126-130m; later update pointed to FY26 EBITDA at the top end and FY27 EBITDA guidance of A$190-200m.
- Recent A$1.85B of contract wins across water, defence, energy, industrial, resources, ports, data centres, health and education.
- Long-tenure contracts with blue-chip/government customers support earnings visibility.
- Company reported 102% EBITDA-to-cash conversion in FY25 and 97% in 1H FY26.
- Balance sheet not stretched: FY25 net cash A$16.2m after Diona integration; 1H FY26 net debt A$21.2m after TAMS acquisition.
- Dividend growth: FY25 dividend 5.5 cps, up 22%; 1H FY26 interim 3.0 cps, up 20%.
- Diversified end markets reduce single-cycle exposure.

## Key Risks

- Valuation risk after rerate: around 14x FY26 EBITDA and ~12x FY27 guided EBITDA is not cheap for services/contracting.
- Execution risk from rapid growth and A$1.85B of new contract wins.
- Contracting/accounting risk: long-term contract estimates, variations, penalties, claims or margin fade can hit earnings quickly.
- Wage and input inflation if contracts are not adequately indexed or priced.
- Acquisition/integration risk from Diona, TAMS and any further roll-up activity.
- Low margin business; small operational misses can matter.
- Working-capital cyclicality can surprise despite recent strong cash conversion.
- Expectations risk after strong share-price rerating.

## Valuation

Current observed data: share price ~A$3.75; shares outstanding ~626.6m; market cap ~A$2.3-2.35B; EV ~A$2.35B; TTM revenue ~A$1.45B; TTM NPAT ~A$55m; TTM EBITDA ~A$120m; TTM FCF ~A$60m.

Multiples: TTM PE ~42x; forward PE likely mid/high-20s on FY26 estimates and potentially low-20s on FY27 if guidance converts cleanly. EV/FY26 guided EBITDA is ~14.0x at A$168m EBITDA. EV/FY27 guided EBITDA is ~12.1x at A$195m midpoint. P/FCF is high-30s on TTM FCF.

Interpretation: the market is already capitalising SRG as a recurring industrial services platform, not a cheap contractor. If FY27 EBITDA of ~A$195m is achieved and the market keeps valuing it at 12-14x EBITDA, the valuation can work. If SRG rerates back toward 8-10x EBITDA, downside is meaningful even with operational progress.

- Evidence that A$1.85B contract wins convert into margin-accretive revenue, not just volume.
- Working-capital performance and cash conversion after rapid growth.
