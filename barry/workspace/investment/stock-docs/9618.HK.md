# 9618.HK — JD.com, Inc.

## Business Overview

JD.com is China's largest supply-chain-based retailer, a 1P (first-party) retailer that buys and
sells goods directly plus a fast-growing marketplace & marketing services business and its own
logistics network (JD Logistics). It is dual-listed (HKEX 9618 HKD / 89618 RMB; NASDAQ ADR "JD").
FY2025 (ended 12/31/25) net revenues were **RMB1,309.1B (+13.0% YoY)**; net product revenues +10.3%,
net service revenues +23.6%. Three reportable segments: **JD Retail** (incl. JD Health, JD Industrials)
≈RMB1,085B revenue with a full-year operating margin of 4.6% (up from 4.0%); **JD Logistics** (RMB217B,
+18.8%); and **New Businesses** (JD Food Delivery, JD Property, Jingxi, overseas) ≈RMB49B. JD runs its
own fulfillment (6.7% of revenue), giving a structural moat versus pure-marketplace rivals but a heavy
cost base. Growth engines into 2026: **Joybuy Europe** (UK/Germany/NL/France/Belgium/Lux full launch),
JD Food Delivery scaling while narrowing losses, and AI integration (50,000+ AI agents internal;
50,000+ JoyStreamer AI digital-human merchants).

## Trait Assessment (Freeroll)

### Downside Support / Valuation Floor — 0.70 / 0.75

- **Floor type:** asset/income backstop plus an in-flight US$5B buyback — not a pure NAV yield floor.
  Yahoo: price/book 1.21x, book HKD95.5; net cash HKD48.3; dividend yield 3.5%. Market cap HK$316.6B
  vs total Dec-2025 equity ≈RMB294B (≈HK$320B) — the stock trades at roughly book.
- **Floor & downside:** a conservative asset/SOTP floor ≈HKD92 (just above the 52-week low of
  HKD95.8), implying ~20% downside if the investment cycle overruns. The buyback (US$5B to Aug 2027;
  ~US$3.0B in 2025, ~US$1B H1'26) is a real price defender; total return was ~10% in 2025.
- **Durability risk:** net cash supports, but total debt is material (RMB107B vs cash RMB225B; quick
  ratio 0.71) and 2025 FCF cratered (RMB6.5B) on heavy capex/investment — the floor erodes a little
  until FCF normalises. Not yet a clean cannot-fall-below backstop; that caps the score.

### Fundamental Stability / No Value Trap — 0.70 / 0.75

- JD is **not** in secular decline. JD Retail grew revenue double-digits in FY2025 and its operating
  margin rose to 4.6%. The 2025 profit slump (RMB19.6B NI vs RMB41.4B in 2024) is largely
  **investment-driven** — new-business spend (New Businesses op loss RMB46.6B), AI, and fulfillment —
  not a melting-ice-cube; FCF rebounded to ~RMB31B in Q2'26.
- But China e-commerce competition (PDD/Temu/TikTok) and macro consumer softness cap the upside;
  Q2'26 revenue declined 2.9% YoY on tough electronics comparisons. Verdict: stable, not impaired.

### Mispriced for an Identifiable Reason — 0.65 / 0.70

- **Consensus:** cheap (1.2x book, 3.5% yield) because the market extrapolates the 2025-26 profit and
  free-cashfall and fears China-consumption macro.
- **Our read:** the profit / FCF slide is dominated by **one-time, planned new-business/AI spend**;
  JD Retail's operating profit still grew in 2025 and the company is returning ~10%/yr to holders.
  Analyst targets (mean HKD152.9, Macquarie HKD180) vs HKD116 price — a concrete gap.
- Caveats: China GMV growth ~3% (July) and a real EU probe overhang (Aug 2026) keep this from scoring
  higher; a first-party retail margin is thin.

### Catalyst — Identifiable, Time-Bound — 0.65 / 0.70

- **Catalysts on a ≤2-year:** (1) **Joybuy Europe** full launch (was targeting Mar-2026 for FR/DE/NL/
  UK/BE/LU) gives a new TAM; (2) **new-business profitability inflection** — Food Delivery narrowing
  losses each Q, JD Retail record 4.6% peak-season margin, FCF rebounding to ~RMB31B; (3)
  **buyback program** US$5B continuing through 2027; (4) multiple analyst re-rating targets.
- **Risk:** the **EU investigation into JD (Aug 2026)** overhang could push the catalyst past the
  window; not trivial. This keeps the catalyst score at ~0.65 — concrete but with timing/probability
  risk.

### Why the other strategies lost

- **Rule Breakers:** JD is mature/single-digit growth post-deceleration; growth/sustainable-advantage
  traits structurally fail the emerging-growth gate.
- **Cash-Cow / Wonderful-and-Fair:** JD's free cash flow generation is weak on the merits (~2.8B,
  huge forward capex intensity), so it is not a cash-cow; the investment-heavy, growth-seeking mix
  doesn't fit a clean compounder.
- **Freeroll is the only one that clears:** a real (asset) floor + an identifiable catalyst.

## Overall

JD is a well-run, asset-rich Chinese retailer trading at ~book with a 3.5% yield and a returning
~10%/yr free cash to ownership — a genuine but not oversized value case. It's not cheap enough to be a
"obvious" buy and the margin is capped by low retail pricing power and a China-macro and EU-probe
overhang. Freeroll verdict: a plausible, defensible, moderate set-up, not an exceptional one.
