# BANB.SW — Bachem Holding AG

## Business Overview

Bachem develops and manufactures peptide and oligonucleotide active pharmaceutical ingredients for research, clinical development and commercial medicines. It operates regulated production sites in Switzerland, the US and the UK, with customers ranging from biotech developers to large pharmaceutical companies. FY2025 sales were CHF 695.1 million: Commercial API is the established production base, while CMC Development feeds future commercial projects. The investment case rests on the shift of peptide medicines into high-volume indications, especially metabolic disease, and Bachem's ability to commission Building K and later Sisslerfeld without losing execution discipline or financial resilience.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — 0.86 / 0.80

Peptides are moving from niche medicines toward mass-market metabolic indications, while oligonucleotides are broadening beyond rare diseases. Bachem's July 2026 pipeline work counted 188 Phase 1, 151 Phase 2 and 94 Phase 3 peptide projects, with four peptide approvals already in 2026; it also counted about 900 active oligonucleotide projects. These are company-compiled figures, but the commercial signal is tangible: customers are prepaying capacity and Bachem expects 35–40% local-currency sales growth in 2026. An independent 2026 IQVIA/Citeline market and pipeline study would raise confidence; add it to Watch.

### Top Dog — 0.82 / 0.70

Bachem describes itself as a leading specialist with more than 50 years of peptide expertise. Its scale, global regulated footprint, Building K commissioning and contracted Sisslerfeld expansion support that claim, and the move toward ton-scale APIs raises barriers for smaller laboratories. However, the company does not publish market share and large CDMOs remain credible competitors. A third-party 2026 peptide/oligonucleotide API market-share study and comparable peer capacity data would raise confidence; add both to Watch.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — 0.78 / 0.75

The moat is built from process know-how, regulatory track record, customer qualification, long production lead times and unusually large dedicated capacity. Management says critical regulated APIs are difficult to substitute in the near term, while customer contracts and prepayments improve planning visibility. FY2025 gross margin was 28.7% and H1 2026 fell to 25.0% as product mix and ramp costs weighed, so pricing power is not unambiguous. The FY2026 annual report's customer-concentration and contracted-volume disclosures would firm up switching-cost and durability evidence; add this review to Watch.

### Durable Growth — 0.72 / 0.85

Revenue grew from CHF 531.7 million in 2022 to CHF 695.1 million in 2025, including 19.2% local-currency growth in 2025. H1 2026 growth slowed to 7.3% in local currencies as Commercial API fell 19.3%, partly offset by 39.5% growth in CMC Development. Management nevertheless guides to 35–40% local-currency FY2026 growth, implying a very large second-half step-up from Building K and commercial deliveries. Growth is real but the guide is execution-heavy, and margins are temporarily declining during the ramp.

### Organic Growth — 0.92 / 0.90

The recent expansion is internally built. The audited accounts show no acquisition additions in 2024 or 2025 and no business-purchase cash outflow in 2023–2025; a CHF 21.9 million acquisition in 2022 was modest relative to the group. Capacity, process development and customer programs—not acquired revenue—drive current growth. Diluted shares rose from 73.7 million in 2022 to 75.0 million in 2025, largely reflecting a 2023 equity raise used to fund expansion rather than acquisition dependence.

### Great Management & Smart Backing — 0.76 / 0.80

Anne-Kathrin Stoller became CEO in 2026 after twenty years at Bachem, including CCO and COO Americas roles. CTO Günther Loidl has been with the company since 2000, while the broader team combines long internal tenure with high-volume manufacturing experience. The Grogg family remains a committed 58.3% shareholder after founder Peter Grogg's death, supporting long-term orientation. Stoller's first full-year execution record and the November 2026 Capital Markets Day will provide the next firm evidence; add both to Watch.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.67 / 0.85

The controlling family has substantial skin in the game and management receives blocked shares. The executive LTI vests over three years and measures EBITDA growth relative to sales growth over a four-year performance period, encouraging profitable rather than indiscriminate expansion. Short-term pay still depends mainly on annual sales and profit targets, and 58.3% family control weakens minority-holder checks; the CHF 33 million related-party loan from Ingro Finanz also warrants scrutiny. Alignment is positive but not pristine.

### Customer Devotion — 0.61 / 0.45

Long-term contracts, strategic collaborations and CHF 142.2 million of customer prepayments at June 2026 show commitment and trust. Yet those signals mix genuine preference with qualification costs and limited near-term alternatives; Bachem does not disclose retention, satisfaction or customer-concentration metrics. Confidence is irreducibly low for now. Revisit after the FY2026 annual report or Capital Markets Day if management publishes renewal, concentration, repeat-program or satisfaction evidence; add that event to Watch.

### Why the other strategies lost

Wonderful-and-Fair is weakened by only 8–10% recent ROIC and by free cash flow being negative throughout 2022–2025 as capacity investment outran operating cash flow. Cash Cow fails outright on free cash generation and is conceptually wrong while the company is reinvesting aggressively. Freeroll lacks a hard valuation floor: the shares trade around 3.8 times book with only a 1.2% dividend yield, and there is no differentiated cheapness thesis.

## Overall

Bachem has an attractive industry setup, a credible specialist moat, organic growth and a long capacity-backed runway. The weak points are a demanding valuation, a 2026 guide requiring exceptional second-half execution, falling first-half margins, negative free cash flow, customer/project concentration risk and middling returns on the enlarged capital base. No universal disqualifier was found in the FY2025 audited report or H1 2026 report.

## Valuation

The Rule Breakers weighted valuation uses only exit-multiple because Bachem has a meaningful, recurring earnings base; a TAM-capture model would add less-reliable market-share assumptions. The CHF 73.65 quote (August 7 close) is paired with CHF 2.45 of normalized current EPS, between FY2025 EPS of CHF 1.98 and CHF 3.03 forward consensus, reflecting the back-half-weighted 2026 guide. The ten-year model assumes aggregate earnings growth fading from 25% to 6%, 0.25% annual dilution, a 22× exit P/E and a 1.22% dividend yield. The script estimates a 10.44% annualized ROI (2.70× over ten years): +12.35% from business growth, −0.25% from dilution, −3.07% from multiple compression and +1.81% from income. This is a growth-carried case with substantial execution risk; `agent_score: 0.66` reflects a strong strategic position offset by capital intensity and a thin valuation cushion.

## Thesis Connections

Exposure to the picks-and-shovels layer of peptide and oligonucleotide medicines, including metabolic/GLP-1 therapies: Bachem supplies specialized APIs rather than bearing individual drug-discovery risk directly.

## Watch

- Track Building K commercial volumes, utilization, batch execution and the Commercial API rebound expected in H2 2026.
- Review the November 26, 2026 Capital Markets Day for post-2026 targets, Stoller's strategy and customer/retention evidence.
- Obtain an independent IQVIA/Citeline peptide and oligonucleotide market/pipeline study and third-party market-share/peer-capacity data.
- Review the FY2026 annual report for customer concentration, contracted volumes, renewals and the economics of the Sisslerfeld collaboration.
- Track 2026 capex against CHF 350–400 million guidance, revolver drawings, customer prepayments and the path back to positive free cash flow.
- Monitor the CHF 33 million related-party Ingro loan and governance under 58.3% family control.
