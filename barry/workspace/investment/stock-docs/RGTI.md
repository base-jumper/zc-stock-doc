# RGTI — Rigetti Computing, Inc.

## Business Overview

Rigetti is a vertically integrated quantum-computing hardware company focused on superconducting qubits. It designs and fabricates chips in-house at Fab-1, provides cloud access through Rigetti QCS and partners such as Amazon Braket and Microsoft Azure Quantum, and sells on-premises QPUs / quantum systems to research, government, academic, and commercial customers.

The current business is still tiny relative to the stock price. Yahoo Finance shows RGTI at **$16.54**, about **$5.5B** market cap / **$5.1B** enterprise value, **~549x TTM sales**, no positive P/E, and **~$418M** cash against **~$6.8M** debt in the Yahoo snapshot. The Q1 2026 release gives the fresher balance-sheet read: **$569M** cash, equivalents and available-for-sale investments at March 31, 2026, with management saying the company ended the quarter with no debt.

Recent execution improved from a very low base. Revenue was **$7.1M** in FY2025, down from **$10.8M** in FY2024, but Q1 2026 revenue was **$4.4M**, up from **$1.5M** in Q1 2025. Consensus from Yahoo is roughly **$23.6M** revenue for 2026 and **$45.0M** for 2027, while EPS remains negative. The company is still burning cash: FY2025 free cash flow was about **-$77M**, and Q1 2026 FCF was about **-$21M**.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — 0.88 / 0.70

Quantum computing is in exactly the kind of large, early, strategically important market that fits the rule-breaker lens. McKinsey's 2026 Quantum Technology Monitor says more than 300 global companies are adopting quantum computing and that the market is moving toward early commercial opportunity; other public market forecasts place the quantum-computing market in the tens of billions by the mid-2030s. Governments, hyperscalers, national labs, and corporate R&D teams are all funding the category.

The score is high because the wave is real and strategic. Confidence is only moderate because the timing remains highly uncertain: useful, fault-tolerant quantum advantage may still be years away, and today's revenue pool is mostly research/infrastructure rather than broad commercial workloads.

### Top Dog — 0.38 / 0.60

Rigetti has genuine technical assets: full-stack control, in-house fabrication, superconducting focus, and a chiplet-based scaling architecture. In 2026 it made its 108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q system generally available across Rigetti QCS, Amazon Braket, Microsoft Azure Quantum, and qBraid. The company also sells Novera QPUs and on-premises systems, including FY2025 purchase orders for two Novera systems totaling about **$5.7M** and an approximately **$8.4M** 108-qubit on-premises order from C-DAC disclosed with FY2025 results.

But it is not the obvious category leader. IBM, Google, Microsoft, IonQ, D-Wave, Quantinuum, PsiQuantum and others have far greater balance sheets, customer access, or mindshare. Rigetti may be a credible specialist, but the public evidence does not yet show it is becoming the dominant platform.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — 0.42 / 0.60

The bull case is that Rigetti's vertical integration, Fab-1, fast superconducting gates, and modular chiplet design compound into a hard-to-copy engineering advantage. The Q1 2026 release cited 99.8% median two-qubit gate fidelity on a 9-qubit system with 40ns gate speeds and up to 99.9% on prototypes, while the 108-qubit system validates the modular direction in production access.

The bear case is equally important: quantum hardware advantage is not yet settled, and large competitors can spend through multiple dead ends. Rigetti still needs to prove that its architecture can scale while keeping fidelity, error correction, uptime, and customer economics competitive. The moat is plausible, not proven.

### Durable Growth — 0.50 / 0.60

There is early evidence of revenue acceleration: Q1 2026 revenue of **$4.4M** was almost 3x the prior-year quarter, and Yahoo consensus expects 2026 revenue around **$23.6M** and 2027 revenue around **$45.0M**. On-premises system orders and cloud availability give the company more routes to convert technology into revenue.

The score stops at mid-range because the revenue base is microscopic and lumpy. FY2025 revenue fell year over year, operating loss was **$84.7M**, and current growth depends on a few systems, research customers, and government/academic budgets rather than proven high-volume commercial demand.

### Organic Growth — 0.70 / 0.65

Growth is mostly organic R&D, product roadmap, and customer deployment. Rigetti is not presenting as a roll-up; its key milestones are new QPUs, cloud availability, system sales, and fidelity/scaling improvements. That is the right kind of growth for a rule-breaker candidate.

The risk is not acquisition quality but commercialization quality: organic technical progress only matters for shareholders if it becomes revenue without endless dilution.

### Management Backing — 0.58 / 0.55

Management has a coherent roadmap: scale superconducting systems through chiplets, sell both cloud access and on-premises hardware, use the strong cash balance to fund development, and expand internationally, including the announced intention to invest up to **$100M** in the UK toward a 1,000+ qubit system over the next three to four years.

Execution credibility is mixed. The team has delivered visible milestones, but the public company history still shows tiny revenue, large losses, and heavy reliance on capital markets. This is a neutral-positive score rather than a management premium.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.25 / 0.60

This is the weakest trait. Rigetti has funded itself heavily through equity: annual diluted shares rose from about **102M** in 2022 to **310M** in 2025, and Yahoo shows **~332M** shares outstanding. FY2025 stock-based compensation was **$17.6M**, more than 2x annual revenue. The company has a strong cash runway now, but per-share value can still leak badly if progress requires repeated equity issuance before meaningful revenue arrives.

### Customer Devotion — 0.45 / 0.45

There are useful adoption signals: availability on major quantum-cloud platforms, Novera QPU sales, on-premises system orders, University of Saskatchewan deployment, and government / research relationships. These suggest customers want hands-on access to Rigetti systems.

Confidence is low because this is not yet customer devotion in the software sense: no retention cohort, NRR, backlog conversion history, or independent customer love metric. The evidence supports technical interest, not yet strong commercial pull.

Other strategies lost quickly. RGTI is not wonderful-and-fair or cash-cow because it lacks durable earnings, free cash flow, high returns on capital, and predictable profitability. It is not a freeroll because the current price is many times cash/book/revenue, so there is no valuation floor or near-term re-rating catalyst that protects downside.

## Overall

RGTI is a real rule-breaker candidate in shape, but not in price discipline. The company has a credible technical story, a huge potential market, and enough cash to keep pushing the roadmap. The problem is that the stock already prices in a lot of future success while today's business is still subscale: **~$10M TTM revenue**, negative operating cash flow, negative FCF, and a multi-billion-dollar market cap.

My judgement: worth watching because the category is important and Rigetti has real milestones, but the current quote is a poor expected-value setup unless you believe the probability of Rigetti becoming one of the quantum winners is much higher than the outside view suggests. I would want either much stronger commercial traction or a much lower price before treating this as investable.
