## Reasoning

By 2013, smartphone ownership had risen from 5% in 2007 to 56%, with a smooth and rapidly accelerating path. A 90% ceiling allows for near-universal eventual relevance while retaining a persistent non-owner segment among US adults. DVD players provide a recent fast consumer-electronics analog; VCRs provide a slower replacement-cycle analog; home computers and cable TV anchor slower adoption and structural non-adoption. The target history is already beyond half of the asserted ceiling and fits cleanly, so the mechanically high fit weight is retained.

## Issues

The eligible analog set lacks a recently matured, directly comparable personal mobile device. The ceiling is therefore more judgment-sensitive than the steepness, and the pre-Pew 2007–2010 bridge estimates add some uncertainty despite the clean fitted trajectory.
