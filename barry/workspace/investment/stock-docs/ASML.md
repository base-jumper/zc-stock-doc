# ASML — ASML Holding N.V.

## Business Overview

ASML is the critical lithography supplier to leading semiconductor manufacturers. It sells lithography systems, software, metrology/inspection and installed-base services that let chipmakers print smaller, denser chip patterns. The strategic asset is EUV lithography: at the leading edge, customers effectively need ASML systems to manufacture advanced logic and DRAM nodes. ASML also sells DUV tools for mature/mainstream processes and an increasingly important installed-base upgrade/service stream.

FY2025 was a record year: ASML reported **€32.7b net sales**, **52.8% gross margin**, **€9.6b net income**, **535 system sales**, **€4.7b R&D**, **€8.5b returned to shareholders**, and a **€38.8b backlog**. Q1 2026 added **€8.8b sales**, **53.0% gross margin**, and **€2.8b net income**; management raised FY2026 guidance to **€36–40b sales** and **51–53% gross margin**, citing AI-driven customer capacity plans and strong order intake. The obvious risk is export controls/geopolitics, especially around China and advanced tools, but the demand signal from AI infrastructure is currently strong.

## Trait Assessment

### Earnings Quality / Consistent, Predictable Profits — 0.85 / confidence 0.85

ASML has demonstrated consistent earning power. Yahoo-derived statements show net income of **€5.6b, €7.8b, €7.6b and €9.6b** from 2022–2025, with no loss years in the available period. Sales grew from **€21.2b in 2022** to **€32.7b in 2025**, and ASML’s own FY2025 release confirms the 15.6% sales increase and record result. Cash backing is strong: cumulative FCF/net income was about **1.0x** over 2022–2025, and 2025 FCF was **€11.0b** versus **€9.6b** net income.

The main caveat is cyclicality in semiconductor capex: 2024 growth was muted and EUV/DUV demand can shift with customer fab timing. I am scoring this as a high-quality but not perfectly smooth earnings stream. Confidence would improve with a deeper 10-year cycle table refreshed from ASML filings rather than Yahoo’s shorter history.

### High, Durable Returns on Capital — 0.95 / confidence 0.85

Returns are elite. The local returns script shows ROIC of **42.4%, 43.0%, 32.3% and 39.5%** from 2022–2025, averaging roughly **39%**, with incremental ROIC around **36%**. ROCE is similarly strong, and ROE is high but not merely leverage-flattered: the balance sheet is net cash.

This is exactly the quantitative fingerprint of a moat. ASML’s installed base, supplier ecosystem, customer co-development and EUV know-how let it earn far above a normal cost of capital. The only reason this is not a full 1.0 is that semiconductor equipment demand remains cyclical and the next wave of High NA/EUV capacity needs continued customer adoption.

### Pricing Power / Durable Moat — 0.95 / confidence 0.85

ASML has one of the strongest industrial moats in public markets. Leading-edge chipmakers need EUV lithography, and ASML is the sole commercial supplier at that capability level. FY2025 gross margin was **52.8%**, Q1 2026 gross margin was **53.0%**, and management still guides **51–53%** for FY2026 while investing heavily in R&D.

The moat sources are hard to replicate: decades of optics, light-source, software and systems integration; deep co-development with TSMC/Samsung/Intel-class customers; supplier dependencies such as Zeiss optics; and an installed base that creates upgrade/service economics. The live threats are geopolitical restrictions, customer attempts to reduce tool intensity, and potential delays in High NA adoption. None currently looks like a near-term moat breach.

### Reinvestment Runway — 0.85 / confidence 0.80

The runway is still long despite ASML’s size. AI infrastructure is driving advanced logic and memory demand; management says customer capacity plans have stepped up for 2026 and beyond. ASML expects EUV sales to increase significantly in 2026, and High NA EUV/EXE systems plus installed-base productivity upgrades give it multiple high-return reinvestment routes.

The constraint is not market irrelevance; it is cycle timing and geopolitics. Export controls can remove demand pockets, and chipmaker capex can pause if AI infrastructure overbuilds. Still, a monopoly-like position in a mission-critical bottleneck with a €38.8b backlog supports a high score.

### Capital Allocation — 0.80 / confidence 0.75

ASML has been a disciplined allocator: it funds heavy R&D, maintains a conservative balance sheet, pays a covered dividend, and repurchases shares. From 2022–2025, the local capital-allocation script shows cumulative dividends of about **€9.9b**, buybacks of about **€12.1b**, almost no acquisition spend, and a **2.3% reduction** in diluted shares. FY2025 alone returned **€8.5b** to shareholders; the new 2026–2028 program authorizes up to **€12b** of repurchases.

The mark-down is valuation discipline. Buying back stock at 40–60x earnings is only attractive if ASML’s long-term growth/returns remain exceptional. It probably can justify some repurchases, but the acid test is not as clean as reinvesting in the business.

### Conservative Debt / Financial Strength — 0.90 / confidence 0.85

ASML is conservatively financed. At FY2025 it had roughly **€13.3b cash and short-term investments** versus **€4.4b total debt**, or about **€8.9b net cash**. The local debt script shows net debt/EBITDA at **-0.71x** in 2025 and near-term debt covered roughly **14.5x** by cash plus a year of FCF.

For a cyclical semiconductor-equipment company, net cash is the correct posture. The remaining uncertainty is detail-level maturity/fixed-floating structure and any off-balance-sheet commitments, but there is no solvency concern.

### Key-Person Risk — 0.85 / confidence 0.75

The thesis does not depend on a single genius CEO. ASML’s advantage is institutional: systems engineering, supplier relationships, customer co-development, patents/trade secrets, installed-base knowledge and a large technical workforce. Christophe Fouquet matters, but the moat is embedded in the organisation and ecosystem. Founder/key-person fragility is low.

Confidence is not perfect because the company is technology-roadmap dependent; senior technical leadership and supplier relationships matter. But losing one executive would likely be a disruption, not a thesis break.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.65 / confidence 0.60

The available annual-report material indicates formal governance and ESG/performance-linked remuneration, but I did not deep-dive the full remuneration report/proxy detail in this run. ASML’s buyback/dividend record and long-term R&D posture suggest management is broadly owner-aligned, while the main risk is the usual large-cap executive-pay gap: incentives can become complex, benchmarked and partly metric-managed.

This is the lowest-confidence trait. Next update should inspect the 2025 remuneration report for LTIP metrics, vesting periods, ownership requirements, shareholding by executives, and dilution.

**Why other strategies lost:** Rule-breakers scores well because ASML is a top-dog beneficiary of AI/advanced-semiconductor demand, but the company is already a mature, highly profitable compounder rather than an emerging disruptor. Cash-cow also scores well on FCF and returns, but ASML still has a meaningful high-return reinvestment runway. Freeroll is the wrong frame: this is not a cheap asset-floor/catalyst setup.

## Overall

ASML is a genuinely wonderful business: near-monopoly economics in EUV, elite returns on capital, strong cash generation, net cash, and a live reinvestment runway tied to AI and advanced semiconductor demand. The business score is very high.

The stock score is lower because valuation is demanding. At the current ADR price of **$1,769.32**, the valuation model implies an entry multiple around the high-40s on my current owner-earnings estimate. That can still work if ASML compounds earnings at a mid-teens rate and exits at a premium multiple, but the margin for disappointment is thin. The biggest risks to monitor are export controls, China DUV exposure, High NA adoption timing, and whether AI-related chip capex proves durable rather than pulled-forward.

## Thesis Connections

ASML is a direct picks-and-shovels beneficiary of AI compute, advanced logic, HBM/DRAM intensity, and the broader semiconductor sovereignty build-out. It is more attractive as a long-duration enabling infrastructure compounder than as a short-term AI momentum trade.
