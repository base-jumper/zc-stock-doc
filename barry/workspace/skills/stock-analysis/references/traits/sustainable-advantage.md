# Trait: Sustainable Competitive Advantage

**What we're looking for:**
The company has something that makes its lead durable — a moat that competitors can't easily
replicate. This could be structural, technical, cultural, or regulatory.

**Key sources of sustainable advantage:**

|Source|Description|
|-|-|
|**Business momentum**|Network effects, switching costs, scale advantages that compound over time|
|**Patent protection**|Proprietary IP, trade secrets, or technology others can't easily copy|
|**Visionary leadership**|A founder or CEO with an unusual ability to see further and move faster|
|**Inept competitors**|Legacy incumbents that are slow, culturally resistant to change, or structurally hamstrung|
|**Brand & trust**|Consumer loyalty so strong that alternatives aren't seriously considered|
|**Data flywheel**|Proprietary data that improves the product as the user base grows|

**Key questions:**

* What would it take for a well-funded competitor to dislodge this company?
* Is the advantage growing or eroding over time?
* Does the company have pricing power?

**Scoring guidance:**

* **0.70–1.00** Multiple clear sources of moat; would be very hard and expensive to replicate
* **0.40–0.69** Some advantage, but it's thin or at risk from a determined competitor
* **0.00–0.39** No clear moat; essentially a commodity player competing on price

**Documentation:**

* Which moat source(s) from the table above apply, with concrete evidence for each
* Evidence the moat is widening or eroding over time
* Pricing-power evidence (successful price increases, gross-margin level and trend)
