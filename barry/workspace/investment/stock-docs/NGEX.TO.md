# NGEX.TO — NGEx Minerals Ltd.

## Business Overview

NGEx is a pre-revenue copper-gold-silver explorer in the Vicuña District. It owns 100% of Lunahuasi in Argentina and about 69.1% of Los Helados in Chile. Lunahuasi, discovered in 2023, is still greenfield exploration and has no mineral-resource estimate; four campaigns have drilled roughly 70,000 metres, defining a mineralized volume of at least 1.1 km × 1.2 km × 1.2 km that remains open. Los Helados is the more advanced asset: at a 0.33% CuEq cutoff it contains 2.08 Bt indicated at 0.51% CuEq and 1.08 Bt inferred at 0.42% CuEq, before NGEx's ownership adjustment. Value creation therefore comes from drilling, resource definition, de-risking and eventual development, partnership or sale—not current revenue.

At June 30, 2026 NGEx had C$463.3m of working capital, including C$81.2m cash, C$133.5m short-term investments and 13.37m LunR shares then worth C$252.7m. It had no financial debt. The balance sheet funds Phase 5 drilling and adit planning, but a mine would require far more capital.

## Trait Assessment

### Right Place and Time — 0.78 / 0.88

Copper has a structural supply challenge: electrification, grids and data centres expand demand while new mines face declining grades, scarce Tier-1 discoveries, high capital costs and long permitting lead times. The stored primary-copper market view estimates first-sale mined-copper value growing from US$300bn in 2026 to US$410bn in 2036. This is attractive, but copper remains cyclical and fragmented; high prices can induce substitution, recycling and delayed demand. NGEx also faces Argentina/Chile permitting and fiscal risk.

### Top Dog — 0.72 / 0.76

Lunahuasi is a standout discovery inside an already important district. Phase 4 expanded the Saturn, Mars and Jupiter zones, found a new zone 300m east of Mars and cut 1,540.1m at 1.17% CuEq, including 88.0m at 8.65% CuEq. Los Helados provides a large defined resource and sits near Caserones infrastructure. The evidence supports a leading exploration position, but no Lunahuasi resource, economic study or reserve yet proves scale or mineability. Confidence would rise with an independent initial Lunahuasi NI 43-101 mineral-resource estimate and subsequent economic study.

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage — 0.64 / 0.76

The moat is geological scarcity and control of a potentially Tier-1 ore system: Lunahuasi is wholly owned, unusually high-grade and adjacent to Los Helados, Filo del Sol, Josemaria and Caserones. The Lundin ecosystem adds district knowledge and strategic options. This is durable if the deposit proves economic, but orebody ownership is not a customer/network moat; value can still be eroded by metallurgy, depth, infrastructure, permitting, royalties and financing. Confidence would rise with Lunahuasi metallurgy, mine-design and preliminary economic work demonstrating that headline intercepts translate into recoverable, financeable economics.

### Durable Growth — 0.74 / 0.78

For this pre-revenue stage the relevant metric is growth in drill-defined mineralization and geological confidence. All four Lunahuasi campaigns have produced significant intercepts; Phase 4 drilled 27,318m in 32 holes, extended named zones and opened new exploration fronts. Phase 5 is planned for Q4 2026, while an exploration adit could enable year-round drilling. Growth is real, but it remains geological rather than economic and cannot yet be measured as resource tonnes or NAV. Confidence would rise with a maiden resource followed by like-for-like growth in tonnes, grade, confidence category and attributable project value.

### Organic Growth — 0.92 / 0.94

The central value gain is drill-bit discovery rather than acquired revenue. Lunahuasi was discovered by NGEx, and subsequent expansion has come from its own exploration. The proposed Valle Ancho spin-out further concentrates the company on Lunahuasi and Los Helados. There is no serial-acquisition footprint manufacturing reported growth.

### Great Management & Smart Backing — 0.92 / 0.92

CEO Wojtek Wodzicki has led NGEx since inception and has more than 30 years of exploration experience; company materials credit teams under his leadership with Lunahuasi, Los Helados, Filo del Sol and Josemaria. The 2025 joint acquisition of Filo by BHP and Lundin Mining and Lundin Mining's acquisition of the 31% Los Helados interest are tangible validation of the team's district record. Nemesia, controlled by the Lundin-family trust, owns 33.97%.

### Incentive Alignment — 0.60 / 0.88

Alignment is meaningful but imperfect. Insiders hold about 37%; the CEO held 2.06m shares worth C$53.0m on May 7, 2026 and must hold five times salary. However, compensation is option-heavy: 11.19m options equal 5.2% of the August 2026 basic share count, and 2025 CEO compensation was C$5.49m, including C$4.19m of option-based awards. Share count rose from 172.1m at 2022 year-end to 216.4m at 2025 year-end as exploration was equity-funded. The 34% family block is aligned economically but reduces minority influence.

### Customer Devotion — 0.10 / 0.95

NGEx has no customers or product revenue. Future copper concentrate should be fungible and saleable if a mine is built, but that is commodity demand rather than customer devotion. This trait is structurally absent rather than merely unresearched.

### Why the Other Strategies Lost

Wonderful-and-fair and cash-cow are vetoed by zero revenue, recurring losses and negative free cash flow. Freeroll scores poorly because cash, investments and book value cover only a small fraction of the C$6.27bn market capitalization; a maiden resource, Phase 5 drilling and the Valle Ancho spin-out are real catalysts, but there is no close, conservatively measured valuation floor or demonstrated mispricing.

No universal disqualifier was found. NGEx has no debt and ample near-term liquidity; audited reporting revealed no identified integrity issue. Royalties, jurisdictional risk, related-party links and concentrated Lundin-family control are material risks, but current evidence does not show an abusive value-leak or equity-threatening liability.

## Overall

NGEx combines a rare discovery record, exceptional drill grades, district positioning, a large defined secondary asset and a strong treasury. The weakness is equally stark: at the current valuation, investors are underwriting substantial future mine economics before Lunahuasi has a resource, study, reserve, permit or financing plan. This is a high-quality exploration option, not a proven operating compounder.

## Valuation

The applicable Rule Breakers lens is TAM-capture; exit-multiple is inapplicable because NGEx has no earnings or FCF. The validated undeveloped-Tier-One-copper-project market document assigns NGEx 1.9% of a US$42bn 2036 revenue pool, or US$798m attributable revenue. This is deliberately Los Helados-only: 69.1% ownership × an analyst-normalized 190 ktpa full-rate case × a 50% commissioning/ramp factor; Lunahuasi gets no production credit without a resource or study.

The valuation uses US$20.742 per share (C$28.91 translated at CAD/USD 0.71746), 228.05m fully diluted shares, a 26% mature EBIT margin and 12× EV/EBIT, anchored to FCX's 2022-2025 EBIT margins but below its high-cycle August 2026 multiple. It assumes 5% annual dilution—below NGEx's 7.9% 2022-2025 basic-share CAGR but still material—and US$500m terminal net debt for development financing. The weighted-average contains only TAM-capture at 100%, because no bottom-up earnings method applies.

The script produces a **−12.66% annualized ROI**: US$2.49bn terminal EV, US$1.99bn equity value, 371.47m exit shares and US$5.36 terminal value per share, a 0.26× ten-year return. Attribution is −8.30% a year from terminal equity being only 0.42× today's market capitalization and −4.76% from dilution. Because the market revenue already probability-weights commissioning, this is an expected project-path return rather than a pure success-case payoff. The mechanical `qv_score` and `cqv_score` are both 0.000 because the methodology clamps negative ROI to zero value.

Current hard financial assets provide only a weak floor: June working capital was about C$2.14 per basic share (C$2.03 per fully diluted share), versus C$28.91 on August 8. The 13.37m LunR shares were worth about C$284.8m at the August 8 C$21.30 quote. The large gap between hard assets and price makes the valuation highly sensitive to the first Lunahuasi resource, project studies, mine timing, ownership, dilution and copper prices; this supports an `agent_score` of 0.30.

## Thesis Connections

Direct exposure to the long-lead-time copper-supply thesis, with gold and silver upside. The Vicuña cluster may create infrastructure and strategic-transaction optionality that standalone projects lack.

## Watch

- Maiden Lunahuasi NI 43-101 resource: tonnes, grade, confidence categories, geometry and metallurgy.
- Phase 5 start in Q4 2026 and whether step-outs keep expanding Saturn, Mars, Jupiter, the porphyry and new eastern zone.
- Exploration-adit decision, RIGI approval, capital cost and schedule.
- Los Helados development path with Lundin Mining, including access, infrastructure-sharing and any economic study.
- Valle Ancho spin-out approval, cash injection and the final value transferred from NGEx.
- Treasury burn, LunR-share volatility, option exercises and fully diluted share-count growth.
- Argentina/Chile permitting, tax, royalty, surface-right and currency-policy changes.
