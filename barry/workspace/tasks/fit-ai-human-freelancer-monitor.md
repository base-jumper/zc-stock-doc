Weekly FIT monitor: AI tasking human freelancers

Read `investment/thesis/AI tasking human freelancers.md` and execute the thesis' "What To Monitor" checklist.

Goal: detect material changes in the landscape for AI agents outsourcing real-world tasks to human freelancers/gig workers.

Check at least:
- Fiverr, Upwork, TaskRabbit/IKEA, Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, PayPal, Block, Adyen, Visa, Mastercard for AI-agent APIs, agent-created jobs, delegated/permissioned payments, escrow, verification, or marketplace pivots.
- Earnings-call/news mentions of AI agents as buyers/customers, automated task creation, AI-to-human workflow orchestration, agent-friendly payments, identity/fraud, or task verification.
- New startups explicitly building marketplaces where AI agents hire humans for physical-world tasks.
- Regulation, fraud, liability, or labour-law developments that materially change the thesis.

Decision rule:
- If there is a material change that should affect Nick's conviction, winners list, or watchlist, send Nick a concise Telegram/current-reachable-chat message with: what changed, why it matters, likely winners/losers, and whether thesis probability should move.
- If nothing material changed, do not message Nick. Record nothing unless there is a useful local note to append to the FIT file.

Important: This is not a weekly report. Silence is correct when there is no meaningful change.
