# Trait: Top Dog

**What we're looking for:**
The company should be, or have a credible path to becoming, one of the dominant players in its
industry. This trait scores the **company's position within the opportunity**: leadership,
market share, category definition, customer mindshare, distribution, product superiority, and
ability to capture an outsized share of the value created by the industry's growth.

**Key questions:**

* Is this company already the leader, or is there a stronger competitor?
* Did the company define the category, or is it following someone else?
* Is its lead widening, stable, or shrinking?
* Does it have the scale, distribution, product quality, brand, data, ecosystem, or execution speed to win?
* If the industry becomes huge, why should this company capture a disproportionate share of the value?
* Could a better-capitalised competitor dislodge it?

**Scoring guidance:**

* **0.70–1.00** Clear leader or emerging leader with evidence its position is strengthening; plausible winner-takes-most or winner-takes-a-lot dynamics
* **0.40–0.69** Strong contender, but not clearly dominant; faces a formidable leader or uncertain competitive structure
* **0.00–0.39** One of many competitors with no clear path to dominance or value capture

**Examples of companies that scored well here:** Amazon in e-commerce, Netflix in streaming,
Nvidia in AI chips/GPUs, ResMed in sleep-apnea devices.

**Notes:**

* Being first matters less than being best positioned now. Amazon was not the first online
  bookstore, but it became the definitive one.
* This trait should be scored separately from industry attractiveness. A company can be top dog in
  a mediocre industry, or a weak player in a wonderful industry.

**Documentation:**

* Market share / rank and its trend — gaining, holding, or losing — with source
* The main competitors, and its position relative to them. Where possible, include figures that help gauge the gap (eg: competitor X has raised 3x more capital; competitor Y has half the revenue)
* Concrete evidence the lead is widening or narrowing
* The specific edge that lets it capture outsized value (distribution, scale, product, ecosystem, brand)
